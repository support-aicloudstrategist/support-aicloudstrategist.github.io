# Day 1 — One-line offer + ICP
Date: 2026-11-23 | Owner: Rajiv

## One-line offer
**Fixed-fee Cloud & AI Cost Diagnostic: we name who owns the spend and what to do next—before you cut, commit, or scale GPUs.**

Primary CTA: Request a diagnostic fit check → `/request-fit-review/`

## ICP (who we sell to)
- **Company:** Product SaaS / AI product company, typically Series A–C or profitable mid-market, AWS/Azure/GCP + Kubernetes and/or GPU spend growing faster than revenue clarity.
- **Buyer:** VP Eng, Head of Platform/SRE, FinOps lead, or CFO/COO when engineering cannot explain the bill.
- **Trigger:** Bill spike, GPU idle waste, shared-platform cost fights, board ask for cost ownership, AI inference spend without a named owner.
- **Budget signal:** Can approve a fixed-fee diagnostic (pilot rate for first 3 seats with anonymised publication permission; rate confirmed in writing).
- **Geography focus (Day 1 list):** India + US/EU remote-friendly SaaS/AI product companies with public cloud footprints.

## Out of scope today
- Pure commodity “we’ll cut X%” shopping
- Agencies needing white-label without a named buyer
- Orgs that will not name a spend owner after the diagnostic
