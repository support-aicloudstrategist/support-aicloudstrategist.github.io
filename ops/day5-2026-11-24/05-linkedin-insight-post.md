## Post copy (ready to publish)

Most cloud “optimization” debates fail the same way.

The invoice is clear.  
The owners are not.

Shared Kubernetes, GPU pools, and multi-brand platforms get averaged into a number nobody can defend—so cuts turn into reliability fights.

Before the next savings program, ask three questions:
1. What spend is **assigned** to a product or team?
2. What is **shared** and needs a real split rule?
3. What is still **unallocated**—and should be investigate or hold?

That is cost governance. Not a percent promise.
