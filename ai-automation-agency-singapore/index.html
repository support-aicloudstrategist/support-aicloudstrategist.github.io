Where Singapore and ASEAN SMEs usually leak revenue

WhatsApp and form enquiries

Prospects ask for pricing, availability, appointments or documents, but there is no owner-visible status, second follow-up or lost-reason capture.

Missed calls and after-hours callbacks

Phone leads arrive during meetings, treatments, classes or after-hours. A callback queue and response SLA can be more valuable than buying more ads.

CRM-lite follow-up

Many SME teams do not need enterprise complexity first. AICS can start with a simple sheet/CRM pipeline, status fields, reminders and weekly owner dashboard.

Appointment and demo booking

Forms, calls, WhatsApp messages and booking links need one workflow for slots, reminders, no-show follow-up and human escalation.

PDPA-aware consent prompts

AI chatbot and voice-agent triage

What a practical Singapore automation sprint includes

  1. Leakage audit: inspect public website, forms, WhatsApp entry points, phone CTA, booking link and follow-up journey.
  2. Workflow map: define trigger, owner, next step, deadline, message template, escalation and evidence field.
  3. Build path: choose simple tools first: forms, sheets, CRM, calendar, WhatsApp Business, email, dashboard or supported automation platform.
  4. Trust boundary: add consent, no-sensitive-advice, opt-out, human escalation and claim limits where needed.
  5. Owner dashboard: show enquiry count, response status, unresolved leads, missed calls, follow-up due and lost reasons.
