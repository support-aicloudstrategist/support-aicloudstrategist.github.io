The costly problem

Technology spend grows faster than ownership.

Provider invoices may show what was consumed, but they rarely settle which product, team or business owner should carry the cost—or which action is safe.

Cloud visibility

Accounts, subscriptions, services, storage, transfer, commitments and shared platforms may be reported differently across providers.

Workload economics

Kubernetes, GPU, model, inference and data-platform consumption may cross product and environment boundaries.

Ownership gaps

Shared or unallocated spend can remain technically visible while lacking an accountable business decision owner.

Action risk

A theoretical reduction may be unsafe when reliability, security, product quality, contract or rollback constraints are not considered.

Evidence standards

Facts, estimates and unknowns remain distinct.

Every material finding should retain its source, scope, assumptions, owner, confidence and unresolved gaps.

01ObservedDirectly supported by supplied source evidence within the agreed period.
02QualifiedSupported but conditional on assumptions, reconciliation or owner acceptance.
03HypothesisA plausible explanation requiring additional evidence before action.
04Owner gapMaterial cost or decision without accepted accountability.

Commercial fit

Qualify the decision before collecting data.

The diagnostic is designed for a material cost or ownership decision—not every cloud bill.

Strong fit

  • Material cloud, Kubernetes, GPU, model or data-platform spend
  • Multiple workloads, products, environments or owners
  • Allocation, ownership or action evidence is incomplete
  • An accountable technology and finance stakeholder can approve scope

May not fit

  • The request is for guaranteed savings or immediate implementation
  • Spend is too small to justify a structured diagnostic
  • Accepted allocation and decision controls already exist
  • No accountable stakeholder can own the decision

Scope boundaries

What the diagnostic does not claim.

No guaranteed savings

No fixed reduction, ROI, benchmark or customer outcome is promised.

No production changes

The diagnostic does not shut down workloads, resize capacity, buy commitments or alter architecture.

No formal audit

It is not an accounting opinion, certification, regulatory assessment or provider-support replacement.

No forced unit economics

If the business denominator is unreliable, the gap is documented rather than converted into a misleading metric.
