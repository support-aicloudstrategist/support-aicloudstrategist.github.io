Approval-gated operating model

Capture the request

Record source, client or prospect, requested action, missing evidence, urgency, owner and promised response before any automation acts.

Classify risk and sensitivity

Separate routine admin, document chasing, account updates, complaints, advice requests, payment issues and compliance-sensitive cases.

Set human review rules

Keep investment, lending, insurance, tax, legal, medical, complaints and final financial commitments under named human approval.

Measure evidence before claims

Track volume, ageing, unresolved-after-SLA, owner acceptance and finance review evidence before making performance or cost claims.
