Problems we solve for manufacturing and export teams

Production follow-up operating model

Capture the request

Convert enquiry, quote, sample, order change, approval, payment and dispatch messages into a structured row with source, owner and next action.

Classify the blocker

Separate customer-waiting, sales-waiting, production-waiting, procurement, QC, logistics and finance blockers so escalation is specific.

Define promise evidence

Track promised date, revised date, reason for change, approval evidence and customer-update status before any dashboard claims delivery health.

Escalate with a human gate

Use automation to surface late or risky items, but keep pricing, shipment commitments, quality exceptions and contractual decisions under human authority.
