Legal intake safety boundaries

No attorney-client relationship by bot

Intake flows can collect facts, route urgency and set expectations, but should not present legal advice or promise representation.

Human review before legal next steps

Matter summaries, eligibility flags and consultation requests are routed for human decision-making.

Confidentiality-aware capture

Forms and scripts should collect only necessary information and keep sensitive details out of public channels.

Conflict-check handoff

The workflow can prompt for parties, location and matter type so staff can complete firm-specific checks.

This page does not claim legal outcomes, clients or attorney approval. It describes an automation design pattern that requires firm-specific review before production use.
