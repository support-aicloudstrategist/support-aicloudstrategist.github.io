ProsperOps alternatives: when savings-share fits and when it does not

Looking for a ProsperOps alternative? Here is when commitment automation fits, when a fixed-fee ownership diagnostic should come first, and what to ask before signing. We are a fixed-fee firm, so read the “when it fits” sections as closely as ours.

The short version

ProsperOps is best known for automated management of AWS commitments (Savings Plans and Reserved Instances), with fees tied to the savings it generates. That is a strong fit for some estates and a narrow lever for others. If you are searching for ProsperOps alternatives, the right answer depends on whether commitment coverage is really your bottleneck.

Questions to ask before you sign any savings-share contract

  1. How is the savings baseline set, and can we audit it?
  2. What happens to fees if usage falls or we migrate workloads?
  3. What commitments are we left holding if we leave?
  4. Does the fee apply to savings we could have made ourselves, such as tagging clean-up or rightsizing?
  5. What is out of scope: shared platform cost, Kubernetes, data transfer, AI/GPU?

When commitment automation is the right call

  • Usage is steady, ownership is clear and coverage is the obvious gap.
  • You would rather pay only when savings appear.
  • You have no capacity to manage commitments in-house.

When a fixed-fee diagnostic comes first

  • Nobody can say who owns shared Kubernetes, logging, data transfer or AI/GPU spend.
  • You want a written decision register rather than automated purchases.
  • You want a plan your own team executes, with no percentage of savings taken.

FAQ

Does AICloudStrategist take a share of savings?

No. The diagnostic is a fixed fee after scope. We do not use savings-share pricing and do not guarantee savings percentages.

Is ProsperOps a bad choice?

No. It can fit well when usage is stable and ownership is clear. The risks to check are an opaque baseline and the issues it does not address, such as unowned shared cost.

Can we use both a diagnostic and commitment automation?

Yes. A common order is the ownership diagnostic first, then commitment automation on spend you have confirmed is owned and stable.
