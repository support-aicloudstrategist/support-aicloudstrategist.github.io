Timeline

Typical cadence after fit.

Single-estate engagements usually run 10–15 business days after complete evidence intake. Complex multi-cloud or GPU estates may take longer; the written proposal states the timeline.

  1. 01

    Fit review

    Day 0–3. Free 30-minute call. Clarify the decision, estate boundary, and fit or no-fit. No billing files required.

  2. 02

    Scope & evidence intake

    Days 1–5. Mutual NDA if needed. Agree redacted exports, retention, and access—never through public forms. Fixed fee confirmed in writing.

  3. 03

    Analysis & ownership map

    Days 3–12. Reconcile spend, name allocation gaps, and classify drivers with confidence and risk.

  4. 04

    Readout & 30-day plan

    Days 10–15. Joint readout. Deliver owned spend map, act / investigate / hold register, and prioritized 30-day plan. One revision cycle included at typical scope.
