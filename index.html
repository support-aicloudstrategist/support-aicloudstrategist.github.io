FAQ

Straight answers.

Is the fit review free?

Yes. A 30-minute suitability call—not the paid diagnostic. The request form takes about two minutes.

What does the diagnostic cost?

Typical single-estate work: USD 2,500–3,000. Complex estates: USD 5,500–12,000. Fee is fixed in writing after scope.

Do you need production credentials?

Not to start. Evidence access is agreed after fit—often export-based or client-controlled.

Do you guarantee savings?

No. We deliver evidence-backed decisions. Outcomes depend on your approvals and implementation.
