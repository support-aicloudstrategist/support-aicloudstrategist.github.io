# Cloud cost alert triage board (one-pager)

AICloudStrategist | Operations brief | November 2026

Seven safe checks before cutting resources after a cost alert. Educational guide — not financial, legal, security or savings advice.

## Seven safe checks
1. Confirm the alert window — hourly, daily, monthly, or reporting delay?
2. Name the service owner — business and technical owner before shutdowns.
3. Separate waste from work — idle, expected, duplicate, or unknown.
4. Check customer and staff impact — human approval for externally visible systems.
5. Look for automation loops — pause expansion before deleting production data.
6. Document the decision — evidence, owners, reversible next step.
7. Review after action — telemetry and owner feedback; avoid public savings claims.
