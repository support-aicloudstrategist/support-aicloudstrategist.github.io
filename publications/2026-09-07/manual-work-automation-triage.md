## Six checks before automation

- **1. Frequency is visible:** Owner question: Does this task happen often enough to justify a workflow? Safe first action: Count occurrences for one week before building automation.
- **2. Input is predictable:** Owner question: Do requests arrive in a consistent format, channel, or form field? Safe first action: Standardise intake before asking software to decide.
- **3. Decision rule is simple:** Owner question: Can a non-expert explain the route, approval, or next step in one sentence? Safe first action: Automate routing and reminders before automating judgement.
- **4. Exception path exists:** Owner question: Where should unclear, emotional, high-value, or sensitive cases go? Safe first action: Send exceptions to a named human owner with context.
- **5. Evidence is retained:** Owner question: Will the business keep the source message, timestamp, owner, and final action? Safe first action: Log the before/after decision trail, not just the task status.
- **6. Customer impact is reversible:** Owner question: Can the action be corrected quickly if automation is wrong? Safe first action: Start with drafts, reminders, tags, and queues before external commitments.
