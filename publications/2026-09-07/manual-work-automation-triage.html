Manual Work Automation Triage: 6 Checks Before You Automate

A safe educational checklist for business owners deciding which repetitive work is ready for automation and which tasks still need human judgement first.

Six checks before automation

StepCheckOwner questionSafe first action
1Frequency is visibleDoes this task happen often enough to justify a workflow?Count occurrences for one week before building automation.
2Input is predictableDo requests arrive in a consistent format, channel, or form field?Standardise intake before asking software to decide.
3Decision rule is simpleCan a non-expert explain the route, approval, or next step in one sentence?Automate routing and reminders before automating judgement.
4Exception path existsWhere should unclear, emotional, high-value, or sensitive cases go?Send exceptions to a named human owner with context.
5Evidence is retainedWill the business keep the source message, timestamp, owner, and final action?Log the before/after decision trail, not just the task status.
6Customer impact is reversibleCan the action be corrected quickly if automation is wrong?Start with drafts, reminders, tags, and queues before external commitments.
