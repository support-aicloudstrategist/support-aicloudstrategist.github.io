# Meeting Notes to CRM Handoff Gate: 7 Safe Checks Before Automating Follow-Up

A safe educational gate for owners and operators to review meeting notes before CRM updates, follow-up tasks, or automation handoffs without making legal, revenue, privacy, or guaranteed-performance claims.

## 7 safe checks

1. **Consent boundary** — Confirm the meeting-notes source is allowed for internal processing before copying anything into CRM.

2. **Identity match** — Match the contact, company, owner, and deal record before creating tasks or updating fields.

3. **Sensitive-data filter** — Remove unnecessary personal, health, financial, legal, credential, or confidential details from summaries.

4. **Promise control** — Separate facts from guesses; do not convert casual discussion into commitments, pricing, dates, or guarantees.

5. **Human owner** — Assign a named reviewer for ambiguous notes, objections, next steps, and customer-facing replies.

6. **Audit trail** — Keep source link, timestamp, automation rule, reviewer, and change reason visible for later review.

7. **Safe next action** — Create a draft task or internal reminder first; send external follow-up only after approved review.

## Verification boundary

Educational operations guide only — not legal, compliance, privacy, security, sales, revenue, medical, financial, approval, or guaranteed-performance advice.
