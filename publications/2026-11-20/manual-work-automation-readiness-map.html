Buyer FAQ

What should be mapped before automating manual work?

Record the workflow owner, trigger, inputs, decision rules, exceptions, expected output, review point and rollback path.

When should a workflow be observed instead of automated?

Observe when the pattern, ownership, decision rules or exceptions are still unclear.

Does this map guarantee a productivity result?

No. It is an educational workflow review, not a productivity guarantee or production-change instruction.
