Healthtech procurement response: an evidence map for AI, GDPR and cloud questions

Use this before a pilot, vendor shortlist or security questionnaire when the buyer asks: “Show me the evidence owner, scope and date.”

Fast answer: keep one dated owner and evidence link for each gate: intended use and risk, GDPR/DPIA inputs, security controls, procurement response, cloud/LLM economics, and the decision/stop rule.

Six evidence gates

1. Intended use

Record the job, affected people, human oversight and what the system must not decide.

2. Data boundary

List personal or health-data flows, purpose, retention questions and unresolved DPIA inputs.

3. Security evidence

Assign an owner, date and source for access, logging, subprocessors and incident questions.

4. Procurement response

Answer only what is evidenced; mark unknowns instead of converting assumptions into commitments.

5. Cloud/LLM economics

Name the cost driver, unit of value, budget owner and change trigger; do not promise savings.

6. Decision brief

State the next reversible step, approver, open risks and explicit hold/stop condition.

Owner review prompts

  1. Who can answer each question without guessing?
  2. Which source is primary, dated and still in scope?
  3. What changes if the model, subprocessor, hosting region or retention policy changes?
  4. What is unknown enough to pause the pilot?
