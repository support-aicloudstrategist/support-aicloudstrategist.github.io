# The AI Change Approval Card

![Infographic: The AI Change Approval Card](https://aicloudstrategist.com/publications/2026-09-04/ai-change-approval-card.png)

Before an AI-suggested change goes live, check the boundary it touches.

## Six approval checks

1. **Customer impact?** Does it change what a buyer, patient, lead, user, or partner sees?
2. **Money impact?** Does it affect pricing, refunds, spend, billing, budgets, or commitments?
3. **Access impact?** Does it touch credentials, permissions, integrations, exports, or private data?
4. **Policy impact?** Does it interpret compliance, security, medical, legal, or HR rules?
5. **Live-system impact?** Does it modify production workflows, automations, routing, or dashboards?
6. **Evidence saved?** Keep source, prompt, draft, reviewer, approval note, final action, and rollback.

Rule of thumb: if any box is YES or UNCLEAR, pause for named-owner review before execution.

Educational operations guide only — not legal, compliance, medical, financial, security, or guaranteed-performance advice.
