# Cloud Cost Alert Triage Board: 7 Safe Checks Before Cutting Resources

![Infographic: Cloud Cost Alert Triage Board: 7 Safe Checks Before Cutting Resources](https://aicloudstrategist.com/publications/2026-09-21/cloud-cost-alert-triage-board.png)

A safe educational board for owners and operators to inspect cloud cost alerts without making risky shutdowns, performance promises, or savings claims.

## Seven safe checks

- **1. Confirm the alert window:** Owner question: Is the spike hourly, daily, monthly, or only a one-time reporting delay? Safe first action: Compare the same service and time window before changing anything.
- **2. Name the service owner:** Owner question: Who understands why this workload, storage bucket, model, job, or database exists? Safe first action: Find the business owner and technical owner before shutdown decisions.
- **3. Separate waste from work:** Owner question: Is this unused spend, planned activity, backup growth, testing, traffic, or a temporary batch job? Safe first action: Tag the alert as investigate, expected, idle, duplicate, or unknown.
- **4. Check customer and staff impact:** Owner question: Could stopping this resource affect customers, payments, support, reporting, staff, or compliance records? Safe first action: Use a human approval line for any externally visible or sensitive system.
- **5. Look for automation loops:** Owner question: Did a script, scheduler, retry queue, AI job, or integration create repeated usage? Safe first action: Pause expansion paths before deleting core data or production resources.
- **6. Document the decision:** Owner question: What evidence explains the alert, the owner, the proposed change, and the rollback path? Safe first action: Record screenshots, IDs, timestamps, owners, and the reversible next step.
- **7. Review after action:** Owner question: Did the change reduce noise without breaking the workload or hiding a real problem? Safe first action: Review internal telemetry and owner feedback; avoid public savings claims.
