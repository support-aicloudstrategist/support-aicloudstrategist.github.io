The six-step handoff

StepAreaRecordSafe instruction
1Name the decisionDecision + ownerWrite the decision this report supports and the person who owns that decision.
2List the sourcesApproved source · Owner · Timing · Access pathRecord each approved source, its owner, update timing and access path.
3Define the rulesCalculation · Filter · Grouping · ExceptionsWrite the calculation, filter, grouping and exception rules in plain language.
4Mark the checksReviewer · Checks · EvidenceIdentify who reviews the output, what they check and what evidence is retained.
5Set the fallbackMissing · Late · Duplicated · InconsistentDefine what happens when data is missing, late, duplicated or inconsistent.
6Choose the release gateManual + Automated → Compare → Owner acceptsRun the automated and manual versions together; release only after the authorized owner accepts the comparison.

Questions buyers and operators ask

What should be documented before automating a recurring report?

Document the decision, decision owner, approved sources, rules, reviewer, retained evidence, fallback, comparison period and release owner.

How should the automated report be checked before release?

Run the automated and manual versions together and let the authorized owner review the comparison.

Does this handoff authorize a production change?

No. It is educational workflow-design material only; local policy and authorized owners control any production decision.
