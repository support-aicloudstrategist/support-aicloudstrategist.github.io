# AI Tool Access Review: 6 Questions Before Connecting Business Data

![AI Tool Access Review: 6 Questions Before Connecting Business Data](https://support-aicloudstrategist.github.io/publications/2026-11-23/ai-tool-access-review.png)

A practical educational review for small-business teams before granting an AI tool access to files, inboxes, calendars, customer records, or internal systems.

1. **Purpose** — What specific task needs this access?
2. **Minimum Access** — Can the task work with fewer files, fields, permissions, or systems?
3. **Data Boundary** — Does the connection avoid secrets, credentials, sensitive personal data, and restricted information?
4. **Owner** — Who approves the connection and remains accountable?
5. **Visibility** — Can the team review what the tool reads, creates, changes, and sends?
6. **Exit Path** — Can access be revoked, tokens rotated, exports removed, and changes reviewed?

**Decision rule:** Connect only when all six answers are clear, approved, and documented. Otherwise pause and ask an authorized owner.
