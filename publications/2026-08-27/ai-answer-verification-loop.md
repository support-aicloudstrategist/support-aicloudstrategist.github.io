# The AI Answer Verification Loop

A safe educational loop for checking AI-generated answers before they influence customers, teams, vendors, public pages, or decisions.

![Infographic: The AI Answer Verification Loop](ai-answer-verification-loop.png)

## Five-step verification loop

1. **Source check:** Confirm which notes, records, policies, URLs, or examples the answer is allowed to use.
2. **Claim check:** Separate facts, assumptions, estimates, opinions, and missing information before sharing.
3. **Risk check:** Pause for human review when the answer touches price, identity, legal meaning, security, health, payments, or public claims.
4. **Audience check:** Rewrite only for the intended reader: customer, employee, vendor, owner, or internal reviewer.
5. **Action check:** Make the next step small, reversible, tracked, and owned by a named human.

## Verification boundary

Educational workflow only — not legal, compliance, medical, financial, security, certification, savings, ranking, revenue, booking, or guaranteed-performance advice.
