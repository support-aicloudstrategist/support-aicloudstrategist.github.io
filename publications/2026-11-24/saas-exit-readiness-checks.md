# Can We Leave This SaaS Tool? 6 Exit Checks Before Renewal

![Can We Leave This SaaS Tool? 6 Exit Checks Before Renewal](https://support-aicloudstrategist.github.io/publications/2026-11-24/saas-exit-readiness-checks.png)

A practical educational checklist for small-business owners testing data portability, ownership, restore evidence, and exit sequencing before a SaaS renewal decision.

1. **Data Inventory** — What records, files, settings, and history does the team rely on?
2. **Export Format** — Can required information be exported in documented, reusable formats?
3. **Relationships** — Do exports preserve links between customers, messages, invoices, tasks, and attachments?
4. **Ownership** — Who is authorized to export, verify, retain, and approve deletion?
5. **Restore Test** — Can a sample export be opened, counted, and reconstructed in a safe test location?
6. **Exit Sequence** — Are access removal, retention, integrations, billing, and final verification ordered and assigned?

**Decision rule:** Renew, renegotiate, or exit only after the required records, owners, test evidence, and sequence are clear.
