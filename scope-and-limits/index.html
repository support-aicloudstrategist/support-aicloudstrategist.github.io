Scope & limits

One offer. Clear boundaries.

AICloudStrategist runs a fixed-fee Cloud & AI Cost Governance Diagnostic. These limits keep evidence honest and implementation in your hands.

In scope

What the diagnostic includes.

  • Owned spend map (assigned, shared, unallocated)
  • Decision register: act / investigate / hold with confidence, risk, and owner
  • 30-day prioritized action plan you own
  • Reconciled cost baseline for the agreed estate and period
  • Readout and one revision cycle at typical single-estate scope

Out of scope

What we do not do.

  • Production changes — we do not execute rightsizing, deletions, or deployments
  • Savings-share fees — never contingent on claimed savings
  • Savings guarantees — no promised percentage reduction or ROI
  • Statutory audit or certification — not a financial audit, SOC report, or pen test
  • Platform resale — not a FinOps SaaS subscription or tool license
  • Invented testimonials — samples use synthetic data; client outcomes only with written consent

Evidence limits

When we say hold.

If ownership or reliability evidence is thin, we classify findings as investigate or hold rather than forcing a cut recommendation.
