## Main Topic
A safe educational brief for defining a small, reviewable AI-tool pilot before a buying decision.

## Learning Objectives
After viewing this infographic, the viewer should understand:
1. Which six areas to define before starting an AI-tool pilot.
2. What minimum evidence to record for a reviewable pilot decision.
3. That the material is educational and does not promise performance or replace specialist review.

## Target Audience
- **Knowledge Level**: Beginner to intermediate business buyer
- **Context**: Evaluating an AI tool without jumping straight to production use
- **Expectations**: A concise pilot-planning checklist

## Content Type Analysis
- **Data Structure**: Six related planning boxes plus one minimum-record strip
- **Key Relationships**: Task and baseline define the starting point; data boundary and human control constrain the pilot; measure and exit decision support review
- **Visual Opportunities**: Six numbered paper cards, simple icons, a highlighted evidence strip and a clear boundary footer
