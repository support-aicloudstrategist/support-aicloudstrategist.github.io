## Style Guidelines
- Warm cream paper background; deep charcoal headlines.
- Restrained Macaron Blue, Peach, Mint and Lavender paper cards.
- Coral Red only for stop/review emphasis.
- Rounded cut-paper edges, hand-drawn arrows, small doodles: flag, person, system window, queue, approval stamp, rule card, question mark, stop sign, clipboard, signpost.
- Friendly educational tone, not corporate stock art, not photorealistic, not 3D glossy.
