# Too Much Manual Work? A 5-Check Automation Readiness Map

## Overview
Use this educational map before automating a repetitive business workflow.

## Learning Objectives
The viewer will understand:
1. The five checks used before an automation decision.
2. The difference between stable rules, judgment calls and exceptions.
3. The evidence and decision choices that bound the next move.

---

## Section 1: Name the workflow

**Key Concept**: Establish a bounded workflow and accountable owner.

**Content**:
- Write one clear start event, one end result and the person who owns the outcome.

**Visual Element**:
- Type: numbered card with start flag, finish flag and owner doodle
- Treatment: Macaron Blue card with hand-drawn outline

**Text Labels**:
- Headline: "1 · NAME THE WORKFLOW"
- Labels: "START", "END", "OWNER"

---

## Section 2: Map the handoffs

**Key Concept**: Show where work changes hands or systems.

**Content**:
- Mark every system, person, queue and approval the work passes through.

**Visual Element**:
- Type: hand-drawn flow with small system, person, queue and approval icons
- Treatment: Macaron Peach card

**Text Labels**:
- Headline: "2 · MAP THE HANDOFFS"
- Labels: "SYSTEM", "PERSON", "QUEUE", "APPROVAL"

---

## Section 3: Check repeatability

**Key Concept**: Separate predictable rules from uncertain work.

**Content**:
- Separate stable rules from judgment calls, rare cases and missing information.

**Visual Element**:
- Type: sorting tray with rule cards and question-mark cards
- Treatment: Macaron Mint card

**Text Labels**:
- Headline: "3 · CHECK REPEATABILITY"
- Labels: "STABLE RULES", "JUDGMENT", "RARE CASES", "MISSING INFO"

---

## Section 4: Protect the exceptions

**Key Concept**: Keep a human stop and review path.

**Content**:
- Define when automation must stop, ask for review or return work to a human owner.

**Visual Element**:
- Type: stop sign, review clipboard and human owner icon
- Treatment: Macaron Lavender card with Coral Red emphasis

**Text Labels**:
- Headline: "4 · PROTECT THE EXCEPTIONS"
- Labels: "STOP", "REVIEW", "HUMAN OWNER"

---

## Section 5: Choose the next move

**Key Concept**: Match the decision to available evidence.

**Content**:
- AUTOMATE when rules and evidence are clear; SIMPLIFY when the process has avoidable steps; OBSERVE when the pattern is still unclear.

**Visual Element**:
- Type: three-way decision signpost
- Treatment: Large bottom card with distinct badges

**Text Labels**:
- Headline: "5 · CHOOSE THE NEXT MOVE"
- Labels: "AUTOMATE", "SIMPLIFY", "OBSERVE"

---

## Design Instructions

### Style Preferences
- Craft-handmade aesthetic with paper-cut cards and simple cartoon icons.
- Minimalist clean canvas, ample whitespace and no complex background textures.
- Deep charcoal text, warm cream canvas and four restrained pastel card colors.

### Layout Preferences
- Landscape 16:9 bento grid.
- Five numbered zones plus a narrow minimum-evidence strip.
- Strong visual hierarchy and large readable title.

### Other Requirements
- English only.
- Footer boundary: "EDUCATIONAL WORKFLOW REVIEW · NOT A PRODUCTIVITY GUARANTEE OR PRODUCTION-CHANGE INSTRUCTION"
- No client logos, customer claims, savings figures, certifications, rankings or compliance claims.
