## Main Topic
A safe educational map for owners to examine a repetitive workflow before choosing automation, simplification or further observation.

## Learning Objectives
After viewing this infographic, the viewer should understand:
1. Which five checks make a manual workflow ready for an automation decision.
2. How to separate repeatable rules from exceptions and judgment calls.
3. Which evidence should be recorded before choosing the next move.

## Target Audience
- **Knowledge Level**: Beginner to intermediate
- **Context**: Business owners and operators assessing repetitive manual work
- **Expectations**: A practical, no-credentials review map rather than a product pitch

## Content Type Analysis
- **Data Structure**: Five related checks arranged as a compact educational overview
- **Key Relationships**: Workflow definition and handoffs lead to repeatability and exception checks, which support a bounded next move
- **Visual Opportunities**: Numbered cards, handoff arrows, exception stop sign, three-way decision badge
