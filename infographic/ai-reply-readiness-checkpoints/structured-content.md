# Structured content — AI Reply Readiness: 7 Checkpoints Before a Bot Answers

## Learning objective
Help operators decide whether an AI-assisted first reply is ready to be sent, needs owner review, or should be routed to a human.

## Checkpoints
- **1. Purpose is narrow:** Owner question: What exact question can the reply answer without guessing? Safe output: A short acknowledgement, routing note, or next-step instruction.
- **2. Source is visible:** Owner question: Which page, policy, FAQ, calendar rule, or owner note supports the answer? Safe output: A reply that cites the known source internally before sending.
- **3. Claim type is labelled:** Owner question: Is this education, scheduling help, opinion, pricing, professional advice, or a promise? Safe output: Education and routing only unless an owner approved the stronger claim.
- **4. Sensitive topic is routed:** Owner question: Could this touch health, money, legal, privacy, security, HR, eligibility, or safety? Safe output: Pause automation and route to the accountable human owner.
- **5. Identity is protected:** Owner question: Does the reply reveal private details, internal notes, credentials, or another person’s data? Safe output: Only minimum necessary information; no secrets or private records.
- **6. Next action is reversible:** Owner question: Can the business undo the action if the reply is wrong or incomplete? Safe output: Draft, tag, route, or ask a clarifying question before committing.
- **7. Human escalation is clear:** Owner question: Who reviews exceptions, complaints, unusual requests, and high-risk messages? Safe output: Named inbox or owner path with a stop rule.

## Boundary
Educational operations guide only — not legal, compliance, medical, financial, security, certification, revenue, savings, ranking, customer-result, approval, or guaranteed-performance advice.
