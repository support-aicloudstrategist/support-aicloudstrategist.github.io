# The AI Output Risk Ladder

![Infographic: The AI Output Risk Ladder](https://aicloudstrategist.com/publications/2026-09-04/ai-output-risk-ladder.png)

A safe educational checklist for deciding when an AI-assisted draft can be used as-is, when it needs owner review, and when it must stop before becoming a business action.

Download the CSV: https://aicloudstrategist.com/publications/2026-09-04/ai-output-risk-ladder.csv

## Five AI output-risk levels

- **Level 1 — Safe note:** Use: Internal reminder, summary, or personal checklist Review: Self-check sources and wording before saving Stop: No stop if it stays internal and non-sensitive
- **Level 2 — Team draft:** Use: Draft for a colleague, task, or operating note Review: Named owner checks facts and missing context Stop: Stop if the draft assigns blame or changes process without owner consent
- **Level 3 — Customer-facing draft:** Use: Website copy, support reply, proposal note, or FAQ Review: Accountable owner checks claims, prices, scope, exclusions, and proof boundary Stop: Stop if it mentions results, clients, savings, rankings, or approvals without evidence
- **Level 4 — Sensitive-domain answer:** Use: Privacy, security, health, finance, legal, compliance, or HR-like topics Review: Route to the right accountable owner or qualified adviser before use Stop: Stop if it could be interpreted as professional advice or a formal compliance/security decision
- **Level 5 — External commitment:** Use: Contract-like promise, spend decision, guarantee, customer send, or production change Review: Formal approval path before action; keep source, reviewer, date, and final text Stop: Stop unless the owner, authority, payment, risk, and audit trail are explicit

## Five checks

- **Source named:** The answer points to a visible source, owner note, approved record, or labelled assumption.
- **Claim type known:** The draft separates education, opinion, internal benchmark, demo, and real approved proof.
- **Risk domain marked:** Brand, customer-specific, privacy, security, legal, pricing, payment, or production risk is labelled.
- **Owner route set:** A named human owner can approve, edit, pause, or reject before external action.
- **Stop rule clear:** The draft says what must not be sent, automated, paid for, or promised without evidence.

**Truth boundary:** Educational operations guide only — not legal, compliance, medical, financial, security, certification, revenue, savings, ranking, customer-result, approval, or guaranteed-performance advice.
