# Structured content — AI Reply Approval Ladder

## Title
AI Reply Approval Ladder: 5 checks before a bot answers a customer

## Learning objectives
- Help owners separate safe routine replies from messages needing review.
- Help teams control promises, escalation, and audit evidence before automation answers customers.
- Keep the guidance educational and claim-safe.

## Sections

### 1. Routine facts
Content: Only automate answers that repeat approved, low-risk business facts.
Visual element: Ladder rung with review gate.
Text labels: Routine facts; approval gate.

### 2. Customer context
Content: Check whether the message includes personal, sensitive, urgent, or unusual context.
Visual element: Ladder rung with review gate.
Text labels: Customer context; approval gate.

### 3. Promise control
Content: Do not let automation promise price, timing, availability, diagnosis, refund, or results.
Visual element: Ladder rung with review gate.
Text labels: Promise control; approval gate.

### 4. Human review
Content: Route uncertain, emotional, legal, medical, payment, credential, or complaint items to a person.
Visual element: Ladder rung with review gate.
Text labels: Human review; approval gate.

### 5. Audit trail
Content: Keep the source message, generated reply, owner, decision, and timestamp visible.
Visual element: Ladder rung with review gate.
Text labels: Audit trail; approval gate.

## Data points
- No statistics used.
- No customer examples used.
- No performance claims used.

## Boundary label
Educational workflow only — no legal, compliance, medical, financial, security, certification, savings, ranking, revenue, booking, or guaranteed-performance advice.
