## Main Topic
A no-credentials, synthetic owner checklist for deciding whether a repetitive small-business workflow is ready for AI automation, rules, or a simpler process fix.

## Learning Objectives
After viewing this infographic, the viewer should understand:
1. Which five workflow questions to answer before buying an AI tool.
2. How to separate repeatable rules from judgement and exceptions.
3. Why the first move should be small, reversible and evidence-matched.

## Target Audience
- **Knowledge Level**: Beginner to intermediate small-business owner.
- **Context**: Evaluating a repetitive workflow before selecting automation software.
- **Expectations**: A safe, practical pre-purchase checklist.

## Content Type Analysis
- **Data Structure**: Five checklist modules plus one decision rule and one claim boundary.
- **Key Relationships**: Trigger and output lead to ownership; ownership and rules expose exceptions; those inputs determine the smallest next move.
- **Visual Opportunities**: Five numbered cards, simple icons, a highlighted decision-rule cell and a boundary footer.

## Key Data Points (Verbatim)
- “Define trigger and output”
- “Name accountable owner”
- “Separate judgement”
- “Write stop and escalation path”
- “Run reversible experiment”
- “No ROI or savings promise”
