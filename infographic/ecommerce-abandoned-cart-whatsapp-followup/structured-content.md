# Structured content — E-commerce Abandoned Cart WhatsApp Follow-up: 7 Checks Before You Automate

## Learning objective
Help store owners inspect abandoned cart follow-up automation safely before increasing message volume.

## Checks
- **1. Confirm consent source:** Owner question: Where did the shopper agree to receive WhatsApp, SMS, email, or cart follow-up messages? Safe first action: Keep a visible consent/source note before adding any automated reminder.
- **2. Separate help from pressure:** Owner question: Is the message useful, or does it feel like a pushy sales chase? Safe first action: Start with one helpful reminder, clear context, and an easy way to stop.
- **3. Check timing windows:** Owner question: When is the cart still relevant without disturbing the buyer? Safe first action: Test a small manual timing window before adding repeated automations.
- **4. Match product context:** Owner question: Does the follow-up reference the right item, stock status, delivery concern, or checkout issue? Safe first action: Use product-safe placeholders and avoid guessing reasons the buyer left.
- **5. Protect support capacity:** Owner question: Who answers if the buyer replies with a question, complaint, cancellation, or delivery issue? Safe first action: Route replies to an owner before increasing message volume.
- **6. Limit discount leakage:** Owner question: Will automatic discounts train buyers to abandon carts to get offers? Safe first action: Separate service reminders from discount rules and review margin impact manually.
- **7. Review evidence weekly:** Owner question: Which messages helped, which annoyed buyers, and which created support work? Safe first action: Track sent count, reply themes, stops/opt-outs, and owner notes before scaling.
