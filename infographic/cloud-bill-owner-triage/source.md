## Six checks before cloud cost changes

- **1. Spike date is known:** Owner question: Which day or hour did the bill pattern change? Safe first action: Mark the first abnormal billing period before touching resources.
- **2. Owner is assigned:** Owner question: Who can explain the workload, data, user traffic, or deployment? Safe first action: Assign one business owner and one technical owner for review.
- **3. Usage driver is separated:** Owner question: Is the increase from compute, storage, data transfer, support, licensing, or AI tokens? Safe first action: Split the bill by service category before proposing changes.
- **4. Business value is checked:** Owner question: Is the spend linked to a launch, customer demand, backup, audit, experiment, or forgotten resource? Safe first action: Tag each large item as keep, pause, resize, investigate, or retire.
- **5. Risk path is visible:** Owner question: Could a cost change affect uptime, security logging, backup retention, or customer experience? Safe first action: Route risky changes to approval; start with reports and alerts.
- **6. Evidence loop remains:** Owner question: Will the team see whether the action helped or created side effects? Safe first action: Record before state, action, owner, timestamp, and follow-up review date.

**Truth boundary:** Educational operations guide only — not legal, compliance, medical, financial, security, certification, savings, ranking, customer-result, approval, or guaranteed-performance advice.
