# AWS bill too high? A 6-step owner action path

## Overview
An unexpected AWS bill is a decision problem before it is a rightsizing problem. Use this six-step pass to identify what changed, who owns it, and what evidence is safe enough for an action.

## Learning Objectives
The viewer will understand:
1. How to move from an unexplained bill change to a named owner and classified driver.
2. How to choose Act, Investigate or Hold based on evidence.
3. The no-credentials educational boundary.

---

## Section 1: Confirm the change
**Key Concept**: Establish a comparable change before acting.

**Content**:
- Compare the current period with the prior comparable period; separate a real usage increase from invoice timing, tax, credits or currency.

**Visual Element**: Paired billing-period tiles with a delta marker.

**Text Labels**: "1", "Confirm the change", "Comparable period"

---

## Section 2: Find the service and account
**Key Concept**: Turn a broad complaint into a traceable scope.

**Content**:
- Record the top changed service, region, account, tag or workload. “AWS is expensive” is not an actionable owner statement.

**Visual Element**: Filter funnel ending in service, region, account, tag and workload labels.

**Text Labels**: "2", "Find service + account", "Scope the change"

---

## Section 3: Name the owner
**Key Concept**: Put accountable ownership beside the spend.

**Content**:
- Assign a product, platform, finance or experiment owner. Mark shared and unallocated spend explicitly instead of inventing a 50/50 split.

**Visual Element**: Owner badge connected to a workload node.

**Text Labels**: "3", "Name the owner", "Product · Platform · Finance · Experiment"

---

## Section 4: Classify the driver
**Key Concept**: Identify the plausible cost driver.

**Content**:
- Check usage, unit price, data transfer, storage growth, logs, managed-service tier, GPU/AI inference, commitment coverage and one-off migration work.

**Visual Element**: Technical diagnostic branches around a central driver node.

**Text Labels**: "4", "Classify the driver", "Usage · Rate · Transfer · Storage · Logs · AI"

---

## Section 5: Choose Act, Investigate or Hold
**Key Concept**: Match action to evidence confidence.

**Content**:
- Act only where evidence is sufficient; investigate where the owner or driver is unclear; hold commitment or destructive changes where confidence is thin.

**Visual Element**: Three-way decision gate with green, amber and slate lanes.

**Text Labels**: "5", "ACT", "INVESTIGATE", "HOLD"

---

## Section 6: Set the next review
**Key Concept**: Close the loop with an owner and stop rule.

**Content**:
- Record owner, evidence requested, decision date and stop rule. A one-time cut without an owner becomes the next surprise.

**Visual Element**: Calendar checkpoint connected back to the owner badge.

**Text Labels**: "6", "Set the next review", "Owner · Evidence · Date · Stop rule"
