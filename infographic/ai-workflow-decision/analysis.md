## Main Topic
A five-check educational gate that contrasts conditions for a bounded AI-assisted workflow action with conditions that require human review before action.

## Learning Objectives
After viewing this infographic, the viewer should understand:
1. The five checks to apply before an AI-assisted workflow acts.
2. The difference between the AUTO-ACT lane and the ASK-FIRST lane.
3. That AUTO-ACT requires every left-side condition to be true and local policy still overrides the gate.

## Target Audience
- **Knowledge Level**: Beginner to intermediate
- **Context**: Business and technical owners designing bounded AI-assisted workflows
- **Expectations**: A quick, safe decision aid without product or legal claims

## Content Type Analysis
- **Data Structure**: Five mirrored checks across two decision lanes
- **Key Relationships**: Each AUTO-ACT condition has a corresponding ASK-FIRST stop condition
- **Visual Opportunities**: Strong center divider, aligned rows, green/teal safe lane, orange/purple review lane, stop/rollback icons

## Key Data Points (Verbatim)
- "AUTO-ACT only when every AUTO-ACT condition is true. Otherwise ASK FIRST."
- "Local policy and authorized human owners always override this educational gate."
- "Educational workflow-design material only — not a security assessment, legal or compliance advice, production authorization, performance guarantee, client case study or customer result."
