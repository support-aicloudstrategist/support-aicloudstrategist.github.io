# AI Workflow Decision: Auto-Act or Ask First?

## Overview
Use this five-check educational gate before allowing an AI-assisted workflow to act.

## Learning Objectives
The viewer will understand:
1. The five checks to apply before an AI-assisted workflow acts.
2. The difference between the AUTO-ACT lane and the ASK-FIRST lane.
3. The decision rule and policy override.

---

## Section 1: Scope

**Key Concept**: Compare bounded action scope with ambiguous or high-impact scope.

**Content**:
- AUTO-ACT lane: Bounded, approved and reversible
- ASK-FIRST lane: Ambiguous, external-facing or high-impact

**Visual Element**: Mirrored rounded cards with a bounded box icon on the left and an outward-facing alert icon on the right.

**Text Labels**:
- Headline: "SCOPE"
- Left: "Bounded, approved and reversible"
- Right: "Ambiguous, external-facing or high-impact"

---

## Section 2: Data

**Key Concept**: Compare approved necessary data with sensitive or restricted data.

**Content**:
- AUTO-ACT lane: Approved, necessary and non-sensitive
- ASK-FIRST lane: Secrets, personal data or restricted data

**Visual Element**: Mirrored data cards with an approved folder on the left and a locked restricted folder on the right.

**Text Labels**:
- Headline: "DATA"
- Left: "Approved, necessary and non-sensitive"
- Right: "Secrets, personal data or restricted data"

---

## Section 3: Evidence

**Key Concept**: Compare evidence-backed rule matching with missing or conflicting evidence.

**Content**:
- AUTO-ACT lane: Rule matched and required evidence is available
- ASK-FIRST lane: Evidence is missing, stale or conflicting

**Visual Element**: Mirrored evidence cards with a check sheet on the left and incomplete documents on the right.

**Text Labels**:
- Headline: "EVIDENCE"
- Left: "Rule matched and required evidence is available"
- Right: "Evidence is missing, stale or conflicting"

---

## Section 4: Review

**Key Concept**: Compare approved logged review with required pre-action human approval.

**Content**:
- AUTO-ACT lane: Action is logged and included in an approved review process
- ASK-FIRST lane: Human approval is required before action

**Visual Element**: Mirrored review cards with a log trail on the left and a human approval hand on the right.

**Text Labels**:
- Headline: "REVIEW"
- Left: "Action is logged and included in an approved review process"
- Right: "Human approval is required before action"

---

## Section 5: Failure Control

**Key Concept**: Compare a ready stop or rollback path with the absence of safe recovery and ownership.

**Content**:
- AUTO-ACT lane: Tested stop or rollback path is ready
- ASK-FIRST lane: No safe stop, rollback or accountable owner

**Visual Element**: Mirrored control cards with a rollback arrow on the left and a stop hand with owner silhouette on the right.

**Text Labels**:
- Headline: "FAILURE CONTROL"
- Left: "Tested stop or rollback path is ready"
- Right: "No safe stop, rollback or accountable owner"

---

## Section 6: Decision Rule

**Key Concept**: Every AUTO-ACT condition must be true; otherwise ask first.

**Content**:
- Decision rule: AUTO-ACT only when every AUTO-ACT condition is true. Otherwise ASK FIRST.
- Local policy and authorized human owners always override this educational gate.

**Visual Element**: Full-width decision strip at the bottom with five checks leading to AUTO-ACT and any stop leading to ASK FIRST.

**Text Labels**:
- Headline: "DECISION RULE"
- Rule: "AUTO-ACT only when every AUTO-ACT condition is true. Otherwise ASK FIRST."
- Override: "Local policy and authorized human owners always override this educational gate."
