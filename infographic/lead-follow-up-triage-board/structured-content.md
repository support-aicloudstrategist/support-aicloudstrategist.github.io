# Structured content — Lead Follow-up Triage Board

## Title
Lead Follow-up Triage Board: 6 morning checks before the inbox gets busy

## Learning objectives
- Help owners review lead follow-up before the workday gets busy.
- Help teams keep enquiries, aging replies, owners, risk pauses, promises, and evidence visible.
- Keep the guidance educational and claim-safe.

## Sections

### 1. New enquiries
Content: Are all new form, WhatsApp, email, phone, and social enquiries in one visible queue?
Visual element: Dashboard card with status badge.
Text labels: New enquiries; morning check.

### 2. Aging replies
Content: Which leads have waited too long and need a human response before more automation runs?
Visual element: Dashboard card with status badge.
Text labels: Aging replies; morning check.

### 3. Owner assigned
Content: Does every open item have a named owner for the next action or escalation?
Visual element: Dashboard card with status badge.
Text labels: Owner assigned; morning check.

### 4. Risk pause
Content: Are legal, medical, tax, pricing, refund, credential, OTP, or sensitive-data items paused for review?
Visual element: Dashboard card with status badge.
Text labels: Risk pause; morning check.

### 5. Promise check
Content: Do automated replies match what the team can actually honour today?
Visual element: Dashboard card with status badge.
Text labels: Promise check; morning check.

### 6. Evidence saved
Content: Are source, timestamp, status, and next-step notes captured for later review?
Visual element: Dashboard card with status badge.
Text labels: Evidence saved; morning check.

## Data points
- No statistics used.
- No customer examples used.
- No performance claims used.

## Boundary label
Educational workflow only — no legal, compliance, medical, financial, security, certification, savings, ranking, revenue, booking, or guaranteed-performance advice.
