Owner-dashboard proof pack

  1. AI system register with owner, workflow type, vendor/model, data category and customer visibility.
  2. Human-review and escalation gap queue for customer-facing or sensitive workflows.
  3. Vendor/data/evidence map with redaction prompts before sharing screenshots or exports.
  4. Adviser handoff list for legal, DPO, privacy, security or compliance specialists.
  5. 30-day operating backlog with review cadence, owner action, blocker and evidence-source columns.
  6. Written approval requirement before any real public proof, logo, testimonial, compliance or result claim.
