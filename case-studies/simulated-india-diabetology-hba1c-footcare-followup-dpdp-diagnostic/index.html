  • HbA1c report photos, old-patient recalls, foot-care notes, refill calls, camp lists and post-discharge reviews sit in one unsegmented follow-up list.

After diagnostic operating rule

  • Each row has queue type, owner role, ageing, evidence gap and next safe action.
  • HbA1c interpretation, medication/refill changes, foot-care and red-flag symptom rows route to clinician review before automation.
  • The result is an owner action backlog, not a DPDP certificate, clinical outcome claim or revenue promise.
