Before diagnostic

  • Pet-owner enquiries sit across calls, WhatsApp chats, Instagram DMs, website forms, referral slips, camp sheets and walk-in registers.
  • Vaccination reminders, appointment requests, post-procedure follow-ups and grooming messages are mixed together.
  • DPDP notice prompts, WhatsApp opt-in evidence, AI/admin boundaries and emergency triage routes are not reviewable as one owner queue.
  • Automation decisions risk accelerating unclear or clinically sensitive handoffs.

After diagnostic operating rule

  • Each enquiry becomes a lightweight evidence row with source, callback timestamp, vaccination state, appointment state, procedure-follow-up owner, notice prompt, opt-in evidence and closure reason.
  • A weekly owner memo shows stale reminders, unlogged follow-ups, missed-call callbacks and owner gaps.
  • Automation is constrained until veterinary, privacy, security and legal boundaries are reviewed by qualified advisers.
  • The result is an action backlog for the owner, not a DPDP certificate, veterinary outcome claim or revenue promise.

Evidence needed before publishing any real veterinary outcome

A real pilot should collect only permissioned, minimized operational exports where possible; define source, vaccination, appointment and procedure-follow-up owner rules; document notice/purpose prompts, WhatsApp opt-in handling, veterinarian-review routes, emergency triage routes and closure reasons; and obtain explicit clinic approval plus qualified veterinary, legal, privacy and security review before any public pet-owner, DPDP, vaccination, appointment, revenue or ROI statement.
