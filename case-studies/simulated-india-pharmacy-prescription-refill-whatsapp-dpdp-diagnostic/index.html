Diagnostic method

What a pharmacy owner can inspect before scaling WhatsApp, AI reception, delivery workflows or CRM follow-up

The diagnostic converts request rows into operating queues: source tagging, owner assignment, callback SLA, refill confirmation, prescription-verification logging, stockout/substitution follow-up, DPDP notice prompt, WhatsApp opt-in evidence, AI boundary disclosure, pharmacist-review route, controlled-medicine boundary and closure reasons.

Before diagnostic

  • WhatsApp refill messages, phone calls and delivery-app spillover are counted as separate queues.
  • Prescription verification is often implicit, so the owner cannot see which refill requests need pharmacist review.
  • Stockout/substitution follow-up sits in memory or chat threads rather than an owner dashboard.
  • DPDP notice prompts, WhatsApp opt-in evidence and AI boundary disclosure are not consistently evidenced.

After diagnostic operating rule

  • Every request source has an owner, status, source tag and closure reason.
  • Refill, prescription-verification and stockout/substitution queues are separated for owner review.
  • Pharmacist-review and controlled-medicine boundaries are explicit before any automation recommendation.
  • DPDP notice prompts, WhatsApp opt-in evidence and AI boundary disclosure become checklist items, not assumed compliance.

Evidence needed before publishing any real pharmacy outcome

A real pilot should collect only permissioned, minimized operational exports where possible; define callback SLA, owner, pharmacist-review and controlled-medicine boundary rules; document prescription-verification logging, stockout/substitution follow-up, notice/purpose prompts, WhatsApp opt-in handling, AI/admin boundary wording and closure reasons; and obtain explicit pharmacy approval plus qualified legal/privacy/advertising/pharmacist review before any public patient, DPDP, delivery, safety, revenue or ROI statement.
