Machine readability was materially incomplete.

Controlled intervention

What this proves—and what it does not

It demonstrates audit discipline, technical correction, revalidation and transparent limitations on AICS’s own website. It is not client delivery, not a customer result, not a ranking guarantee, and not evidence of revenue, bookings, reviews, savings or enterprise production operation.
