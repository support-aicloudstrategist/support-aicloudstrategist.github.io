FAQ

Questions buyers ask before the call.

Clear commercial answers—so the fit review stays about the decision, not the mechanics.

Is the fit review really free?

Yes. It is a short suitability conversation—not a free diagnostic and not a findings deliverable from confidential billing data.

When is the fee locked?

After fit review, in a written proposal that states estate boundary, period and fixed fee—before sensitive evidence is transferred.

Why no monthly / annual toggle?

This is a bounded diagnostic, not a SaaS subscription. You pay a fixed fee for one engagement—not a recurring seat or usage plan.

Do you take a percentage of savings?

No. Never contingent on claimed savings. That model distorts evidence and ownership decisions.

Do you need production credentials?

Not through the website. If we proceed, evidence access is agreed first—often export-based or client-controlled.

Do you guarantee savings?

No. We deliver evidence-backed decisions and safe next actions. Outcomes depend on your approvals and implementation.
