Where Spanish SMEs, clinics and service firms usually leak growth

WhatsApp enquiries with no second touch

Prospects message the business, but there is no clear owner, SLA, follow-up status, opt-out note or lost-reason capture.

Missed calls and after-hours callbacks

Calls arrive during appointments, site visits, siesta hours, production work or evenings. A callback queue and response rule can protect demand already created.

CRM follow-up without discipline

HubSpot, Pipedrive, Zoho, Salesforce or spreadsheets may exist, but stages, reminders and owner dashboards are still inconsistent.

Appointment and consultation booking

Private clinics, consultancies, academies and local services need booking links, reminders, callback ownership and careful human escalation in one workflow.

GDPR/LOPDGDD-aware operational prompts

Workflows should include purpose, consent, opt-out, retention and escalation prompts. This is operational readiness guidance, not legal advice.

AI triage with clear boundaries

Chatbots and voice agents should collect context and route work, not provide unsupported medical, legal, financial, compliance or revenue advice.
