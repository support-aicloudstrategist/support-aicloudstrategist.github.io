# Gold-Standard Commercial Service Page Template — AICloudStrategist

Purpose: create fewer, stronger commercial pages that generate qualified enquiries and revenue. Do not use this to mass-generate pages. Use it to rewrite one page at a time until it can honestly compete.

## Completion gate
A commercial service page is not complete until it is:
- Implemented in repository
- Committed
- Pushed
- Deployed to production
- Verified live with HTTP 200, title, H1, canonical, schema, sitemap entry and internal links
- Compared against current top competitors
- Scored honestly for quality and ranking readiness

## Target quality bar
- 1,500–2,500 useful words, not filler
- Clear primary keyword and 3–6 supporting commercial terms
- Global by default; regional modifiers only where intentional
- Strong buyer intent: who it is for, what problem it solves, what outcome it creates
- Original frameworks, checklists, workflows, diagrams or examples
- Practical implementation detail, not vague “AI-powered” claims
- EEAT: experience, methodology, proof boundaries, credentials, author/reviewer or operator context
- Clear limitations and human approval boundaries for AI systems
- Specific CTA aligned to the page intent

## Required page structure
1. Hero
   - H1 with buyer-intent keyword
   - Specific promise without hype
   - Primary CTA and secondary CTA
   - Trust statement / proof boundary

2. Buyer problem section
   - Explain the real operational or revenue leakage
   - Include symptoms buyers recognize
   - State when the service is not the right fit

3. Outcomes and business impact
   - 4–6 concrete outcomes
   - Tie each outcome to revenue, cost reduction, conversion, speed, risk reduction or visibility

4. Service scope
   - Discovery/audit
   - Design/workflow mapping
   - Build/integration
   - Testing/approval gates
   - Training/documentation
   - Measurement/iteration

5. Original framework
   - Name an AICloudStrategist framework or operating model
   - Include steps, scoring matrix, maturity model or decision tree

6. Use cases
   - At least 5 concrete use cases
   - Each must include trigger, workflow, tools/systems, human handoff and success metric

7. Implementation roadmap
   - Day 0–7, week 2–3, week 4+, ongoing optimization
   - What client needs to provide
   - What AICloudStrategist delivers

8. Integrations and technical approach
   - Websites/forms, WhatsApp, CRM, email, calendar, cloud/analytics, databases or APIs as relevant
   - Privacy/security considerations

9. Risks and safeguards
   - AI hallucination, privacy, consent, escalation, data quality, vendor lock-in, operational adoption
   - Explain controls honestly

10. Proof / examples
   - Link to real case study if available
   - If only demo/benchmark exists, label it clearly
   - Include before/after workflow or sample scenario

11. Pricing drivers
   - Explain what changes effort/cost without publishing fake prices
   - Include small / standard / complex scope examples if possible

12. FAQs
   - 6–10 FAQs based on buyer objections and search intent
   - Use FAQ schema only when visible FAQs exist

13. Internal links
   - Link up to parent hub, related services, relevant resources, relevant industries, proof pages and assessment CTA
   - Include contextual editorial links, not only footer cards

14. CTA block
   - Service-specific CTA, e.g. “Map your AI automation opportunities”
   - Explain what happens after enquiry

## Scoring rubric
- 9–10: Ready to compete; strong original value, proof, depth, CTA and internal links
- 7–8: Good foundation; publishable but needs more proof/data/visuals
- 5–6: Needs improvement; useful but not competitive
- 1–4: Rewrite required; thin, generic, repetitive or weak intent match
