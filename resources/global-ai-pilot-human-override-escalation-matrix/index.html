Minimum controls before production scale

Define the boundary

  • What AI outputs are advisory only versus action-triggering?
  • Which roles can approve, override, pause or roll back the pilot?
  • Which customer, financial, clinical, legal, hiring or security decisions are excluded?
  • What wording is allowed for internal and external capability claims?

Make oversight auditable

  • Keep override reason codes short enough for operators to use.
  • Store examples of blocked, approved and corrected outputs.
  • Review override trends before expanding users, automations or data scope.
  • Connect unresolved items to a risk register and dated owner queue.
