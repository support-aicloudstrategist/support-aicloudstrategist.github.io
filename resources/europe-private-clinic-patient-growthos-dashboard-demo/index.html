What this dashboard proves about the AICS method

Source-to-owner queue

Every web form, call, marketplace booking, WhatsApp click, referral email and no-show row has a named operational owner and age bucket.

GDPR adviser questions

The dashboard separates operational follow-up from questions that need qualified privacy/legal review: consent notices, retention, processors, transfer routes and access/deletion routing.

AI boundary routing

AI receptionist or automation candidates are labelled with human-review triggers where symptoms, minors, vulnerable patients, complaints, pricing/suitability or clinical judgement may appear.

Decision-ready backlog

Rows become callback, rebooking, blocker, escalation, consent-review, automation-candidate or no-action decisions instead of generic software feature requests.
