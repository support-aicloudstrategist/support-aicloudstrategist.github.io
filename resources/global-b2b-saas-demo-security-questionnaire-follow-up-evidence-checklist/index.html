The 10 evidence checks before automating stalled SaaS demo follow-up

1. Security-questionnaire intake log

Track questionnaire source, due date, buyer role, topic category and accountable response owner without exposing customer names or deal records by default.

2. Approved answer source map

Connect every answer to an approved source owner, evidence link category and freshness date instead of copying from unreviewed old responses.

3. AI-use boundary

Separate product AI features, internal AI assistants, training-use statements, human review and prohibited claims before sending buyer-facing answers.

4. DPA/MSA blocker ownership

Show whether legal, privacy, security, finance, procurement or executive review owns the next step and what evidence is safe to share.

5. Trust-centre gap register

List requested artefacts such as SOC 2, ISO, subprocessor, penetration test, BAA, SSO, encryption, residency or incident-response evidence as gaps or approved references.

6. Demo-to-pilot handoff

Document who owns the buyer follow-up after the technical demo: AE, SE, security, CS, founder, legal or deal desk.

7. Unsafe external answer stop list

Escalate compliance guarantees, legal positions, security certifications, privacy promises, AI accuracy, ROI and customer outcome claims to qualified owners.

8. Redacted evidence pack

Use anonymised fields and synthetic examples for review; do not export prospects, contracts, tickets, access logs, pricing exceptions or confidential security documents by default.

9. Deal ageing and next action

Make stalled questionnaires visible by age, owner, blocker, approved next action and follow-up channel rather than leaving them buried in inboxes.

10. Claim approval gate

Hold public claims about shorter sales cycles, security readiness, revenue impact, win rate, compliance or customer outcomes unless approved owners have documented proof.
