US patient acquisition leakage checklist

Speed-to-lead by route

Test phone, form, booking request, chat, email, SMS, Google Business Profile and paid landing pages. Record response time, owner and backup path.

Intake handoff visibility

Track what happens between a lead arriving and the appointment being scheduled, cancelled, rescheduled or marked not-fit.

HIPAA-aware workflow questions

Do not collect unnecessary protected health details in casual forms or unmanaged chat threads. Check access control, minimum necessary data, retention and escalation rules with qualified advisers.

Information blocking awareness

For healthtech and provider teams, align operational workflows with qualified guidance on patient access and information blocking obligations. This page is not legal advice.

FAQ

Does Healthcare GrowthOS replace Phreesia, Tebra, Luma, NexHealth or Zocdoc?

No. It is a leakage and operating-system layer around existing systems, not a replacement for patient engagement, EHR, booking marketplace, billing or practice management software.

Is this HIPAA legal advice?

No. AICS does not provide legal advice or HIPAA certification. This is operational readiness guidance for better workflow questions, safer intake paths and clearer ownership.

Why not just buy more patient leads?

More spend can hide operational failure. Fix the owner-visible path from enquiry to scheduled appointment first, then scale demand with better confidence.
