Scale

Only the narrow workflow with approved data boundaries, named owners and monitored thresholds should be considered for controlled expansion.

Remediate

Reliability, cost-owner and rollback gaps should move into a dated remediation decision log before the next board update.

Pause

Any unresolved sensitive-data, regulated-use, security, safety or claim-approval blocker should pause broad rollout and public claims.
