SaaS exit readiness: check the evidence before renewal

Use this owner path when a team is asking, “Can we leave this SaaS tool safely?” It turns a renewal decision into six inspectable checks before access is removed or a replacement is chosen.

  1. 1. InventoryList the records, files, settings, history and attachments the team actually relies on.
  2. 2. Export formatConfirm which required information can be exported in documented, reusable formats.
  3. 3. RelationshipsTest whether exports preserve links between customers, messages, invoices, tasks and attachments.
  4. 4. OwnershipAssign who may export, verify, retain and approve deletion or access removal.
  5. 5. Restore testOpen, count and reconstruct a sample export in a safe test location before treating it as usable.
  6. 6. Exit sequenceOrder retention, integrations, billing, access removal and final verification, with an owner for each step.

Decision rule

Renew, renegotiate or exit only after the required records, owners, test evidence and sequence are clear. If a check is unresolved, hold the irreversible step and document the missing evidence.

Buyer-safe handoff

If the team wants a scoped fit review, share the decision question, a redacted evidence summary, the unresolved boundary and the smallest reversible next step. Do not send credentials, production exports, personal data or regulated records just to determine fit.

  • Decision: what renewal, exit or replacement choice is being considered?
  • Evidence: what was checked, by whom and in what safe test context?
  • Boundary: what is unavailable, sensitive or still unverified?
  • Next step: what bounded review can happen without changing production?

Common buyer questions

Can we leave this SaaS tool safely?

Only treat the exit as ready after the required records, export format, relationships, accountable owner, restore test and exit sequence are inspectable.

What should we check before a SaaS renewal decision?

Check inventory, export usability, linked records and attachments, ownership, a safe restore test and the order of retention, integrations, billing and access removal.

What if one SaaS exit check is unresolved?

Hold the irreversible step, record the missing evidence and assign an owner rather than treating an untested export or undocumented sequence as ready.
