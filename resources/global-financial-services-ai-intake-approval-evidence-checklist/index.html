Why this exists before another workflow, CRM or AI tool

Financial services teams often already have email, spreadsheets, portals, CRMs, document tools, call logs and ticket queues. The commercial gap is proving which client requests, document chases, risk reviews, renewals, proposals and cost questions are stuck without an accountable owner or approval trail.

What AICS must publish/build to enter top-3/top-5 consideration

AICS should be judged on evidence-first operating design: intake source mapping, document status visibility, sensitivity flags, prohibited-advice boundaries, approval ladders, risk-review queue ageing and owner-ready dashboards before AI-assisted workflows are trusted.

Evidence fields to capture

  • Request source: website form, email, call, WhatsApp/SMS, portal task, adviser referral, renewal list, customer support queue, partner referral or internal exception.
  • Financial workflow type: onboarding, KYC, loan file, policy or benefits renewal, advisory proposal, bookkeeping documents, invoice query, payment issue, complaint, risk review or cloud/AI spend approval.
  • Document state: requested, received, incomplete, identity check required, client clarification needed, internal review due, adviser/legal/compliance review due or closed.
  • Sensitivity and boundary: personal data, financial records, regulated advice request, complaint, lending decision, investment decision, insurance coverage question, tax/legal issue or payment/collections issue.
  • AI role allowed: summarize source evidence, classify queue, draft staff-reviewed reminder, extract missing-document fields or prepare dashboard notes; never final advice, eligibility, approval, policy interpretation or financial commitment.
  • Owner and ageing: relationship manager, adviser, loan officer, producer, accountant, operations lead, risk/compliance owner, finance owner; same day, 1-2 days, 3-7 days or older than 7 days.
  • Approval evidence: named reviewer, source used, decision logged, exception accepted, escalation owner, next client action and next review date.
  • Closure reason: completed, client unreachable, document missing, adviser pending, risk/compliance pending, finance pending, duplicate, not in scope, declined or cancelled.

Owner dashboard rows

  • Open client requests by source, workflow type, owner and age.
  • Document chases with no response evidence before SLA.
  • AI-drafted items awaiting human review or approval.
  • Risk, complaint, advice, payment or compliance-sensitive items requiring escalation.
  • Cloud, AI API or automation spend awaiting finance owner acceptance.
  • Closed items with incomplete evidence or unclear closure reason.

Safe AI and automation boundaries

  • AI can organize evidence, summarize documents, classify queue state and draft internal notes for review.
  • AI should not provide investment advice, lending approval, insurance advice, legal advice, tax advice, complaint resolution, payment demand or final client commitment.
  • Regulated, sensitive, identity, complaint and financial-impact decisions need named human approval.
  • Measure evidence completeness before claiming speed, conversion, revenue, retention, compliance, savings or ROI improvement.

Diagnostic outputs AICS can safely provide

  • Client-intake source-to-owner leakage map.
  • Document-chasing status and ageing register.
  • AI usage boundary and human-review matrix.
  • Risk-review and exception queue dashboard outline.
  • Cloud/AI spend owner and approval cadence map.
  • First 30-day operating review checklist for accepted workflows.
