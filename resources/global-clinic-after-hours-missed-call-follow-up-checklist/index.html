Evidence checklist before buying another reception or automation tool

1. After-hours missed-call register

Track calls by source, day, time, specialty, language, likely urgency, callback owner and next step instead of only counting total missed calls.

2. Appointment-request queue

Separate new consultations, follow-ups, report queries, procedure questions, cancellations, reschedules and second-opinion requests so the clinic can see what is still open.

3. WhatsApp and form handoff

Map whether WhatsApp messages, web forms, Google Business Profile calls, ad enquiries and receptionist notes reach the same appointment queue.

4. Human-review boundary

Keep symptoms, emergencies, clinical questions, consent/privacy uncertainty, complaints, refunds and insurance exceptions under staff review.

5. Leakage reason tags

Tag unreachable patients, unavailable slots, price questions, missing doctor preference, no referral details, language mismatch and incomplete contact information.

6. Weekly owner dashboard

Show after-hours calls, callbacks due, appointments booked, no-response items, staff handoffs and ageing by source before increasing ad spend.

Use this as a proof-before-platform filter

If the clinic cannot see which after-hours calls, appointment requests and handoffs are still open, another phone line, CRM or AI receptionist may make leakage harder to spot.
