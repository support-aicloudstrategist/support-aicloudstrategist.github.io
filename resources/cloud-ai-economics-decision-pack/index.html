04 · Cost-driver assessment

Growth, waste and uncertainty stay separate.

A

Useful demand

Consumption associated with accepted growth or workload demand. It is not labelled waste merely because cost increased.

B

Optimization candidate

A reversible action with sufficient workload, owner and constraint evidence for bounded validation.

C

Evidence gap

A material cost signal that cannot yet support action because allocation, owner or technical context is incomplete.

06 · 30-day action plan

A client-owned sequence, not an implementation promise.

  1. Days 1–5

    Accept the cost boundary

    Confirm included services, period, currency treatment, shared pools and known exclusions.

  2. Days 6–10

    Resolve priority owner gaps

    Assign accountable roles to material unallocated and shared cost pools.

  3. Days 11–18

    Validate bounded actions

    Test reversible options against workload, reliability, security and commercial constraints.

  4. Days 19–24

    Approve or hold decisions

    Record authority, dependency, rollback condition and measurement rule.

  5. Days 25–30

    Establish the control loop

    Define review cadence, anomaly ownership and the evidence required to verify any implemented action.
