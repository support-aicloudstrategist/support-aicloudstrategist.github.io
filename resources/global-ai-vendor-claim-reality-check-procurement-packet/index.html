What the packet checks before spend

Claim evidence

Separate live proof, demo assumptions, benchmark claims, model limits and unsupported revenue, savings, ranking or accuracy promises.

Data and access boundary

Confirm whether the vendor needs production data, credentials, customer records, PHI/ePHI, personal data or broad system access before approval.

Human handoff and lock-in

Check escalation paths, failure ownership, export options, support limits, change control and cancellation or portability evidence.
