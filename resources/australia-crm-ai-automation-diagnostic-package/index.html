Diagnostic scope

1. Enquiry-source inventory

Map website forms, missed calls, booking links, webchat, email, ads, Google Business Profile, social DMs and referral messages into one owner-readable queue.

2. Response-SLA ownership

Identify who must respond, when follow-up is late, what should be escalated to a human and what can be safely templated.

3. CRM evidence fields

Define minimum fields for source, status, consent, next action, owner, ageing, blocker and closure reason before automation is added.

4. AI automation boundary

Separate safe reminders and routing from price promises, regulated advice, identity issues, complaints, refunds and sensitive personal information.

Outputs

  • Redacted enquiry-source and follow-up leakage map.
  • Owner-ready CRM field checklist and dashboard wireframe.
  • APP-aware operational prompt questions for qualified owner review.
  • Unsafe-claim stop list covering legal, privacy, compliance, lead, appointment, revenue, ranking and ROI claims.
  • Next-step recommendation: clean CRM, workflow automation, AI receptionist/chatbot, agency handoff or no-build fix.
