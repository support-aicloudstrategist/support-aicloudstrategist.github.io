Five-question hold rule

Do not automate the fog

  1. Can one owner describe the current workflow end to end?
  2. Are the inputs allowed to enter the proposed system?
  3. What happens when the rule is uncertain or wrong?
  4. Can a person review, reverse or stop the action?
  5. What is the smallest reversible test?
Forwardable next step

Write one workflow, one owner, one exception path and one stop condition. If any is missing, pause tool selection and resolve that gap first.
