FAQ

Should a SaaS company buy AICS instead of a trust-centre or questionnaire automation product?

Not necessarily. If the main pain is answer reuse or external document sharing, a dedicated platform may be the right tool. AICS is for the operational evidence gap behind the answers.

Can AICS write final legal or compliance answers?

No. AICS can organize facts and draft operational evidence packs for review, but legal, privacy, security, audit, DPO and certification decisions require qualified professionals.
