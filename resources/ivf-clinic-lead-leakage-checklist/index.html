The ten checks before buying more IVF ads, CRM, WhatsApp automation or AI receptionist software

1. Missed-call capture

Separate answered calls, missed calls, abandoned calls, after-hours calls, duplicate numbers and callbacks so paid demand is not hidden inside phone logs.

2. WhatsApp and form source map

Tag Google Business Profile, paid search, Meta ads, website forms, WhatsApp buttons, referrals, doctor networks and marketplace leads before judging campaign quality.

3. Counsellor ownership

Every enquiry needs a named counsellor or front-desk owner, backup owner, due time and escalation path if the patient query is sensitive or urgent.

4. First-response SLA

Track whether new enquiries receive a safe acknowledgement and callback queue entry within the clinic’s chosen service window, without promising clinical outcomes.

5. Appointment handoff

Record whether the enquiry moved to consultation booked, doctor review, diagnostic/lab question, finance discussion, not suitable, no response or owner review.

6. Consent and notice evidence

Check that contact purpose, opt-out handling, privacy notice prompts and sensitive-health-data boundaries are reviewed by qualified local advisers before automation.

7. AI receptionist boundary

Define which questions can be acknowledged, which need human review and which must route to a doctor, nurse, embryologist or qualified staff member.

8. No-show and reschedule loop

Track missed consultations, reschedule requests, reminders, reason codes and recovery owner instead of only counting booked appointments.

9. Campaign-to-consult visibility

Compare campaign source, enquiry type, first response, counsellor owner, appointment status and closure reason in one owner dashboard.

10. Proof pack for management review

Prepare an evidence pack showing leakage categories, workflow gaps, scripts needing approval and a 30-day backlog before committing to a larger build.

FAQ

Should an IVF clinic buy more ads first?

Not always. If missed calls, WhatsApp replies, counsellor ownership and appointment handoffs are unclear, more ads can create more leakage instead of more booked consultations.

Can AI answer fertility patient questions?

Only within clinic-approved boundaries. Sensitive medical, treatment, pricing, medication, lab, eligibility and urgent questions should route to qualified human staff under local rules.
