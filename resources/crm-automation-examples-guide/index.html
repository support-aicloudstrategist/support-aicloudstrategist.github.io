CRM automation examples for service businesses

Seven practical CRM automation examples

1. Missed-call to callback queue

Every missed call creates a record with source, callback owner, urgency, response due time and open/closed status.

2. WhatsApp enquiry capture

New WhatsApp enquiries are logged with service interest, consent prompt status, owner and the next promised follow-up.

3. Appointment no-show recovery

No-shows move into a human-reviewed queue with safe reminder language, channel preference and rebooking status.

4. Quote follow-up pipeline

Quotes get stage, value band, blocker, next action and review date fields so owners can see ageing opportunities.

5. Renewal reminder workflow

Renewals trigger reminders with account owner, last contact, risk note and human approval for sensitive messages.

6. Lead source quality review

Website, ads, referrals, marketplace and offline leads are tagged so the owner can compare volume, quality and follow-up gaps.

7. Exception handoff

Complaints, pricing disputes, medical/legal/financial questions and missing-context items are routed to a human before any promise is sent.

Human-review flagKeeps sensitive, risky or unclear replies away from unsupervised automation.
