Research snapshot: Europe business morning

Public competitor pages reviewed during this run showed the buyer-language AICS must meet. Accurx leads with “the simple way to communicate about patient care” and poor communication. Pabau positions an all-in-one practice platform covering calendar, online booking, EMR, workflows, payments, marketing, growth insights, AI and a trust centre. Semble positions a complete healthcare management platform for practice management, EHR, intelligent workflows, patient experience, scheduling, integrations and trust. Cliniko positions practice-management software for clinics and allied-health practitioners with schedule, treatment records, invoices, payments, transparent pricing, security and connected apps. Doctolib is a recognised European booking/patient-journey reference, but its site blocked automated review during this run.

What AICS must publish/build to enter top-3/top-5 consideration

  • More proof-before-platform assets: dashboard screenshots, synthetic CSV examples and redacted evidence-room structures, clearly labelled as demo unless real permission exists.
  • Comparison language: pages that meet “alternative”, “vs”, “patient engagement”, “AI receptionist”, “practice management” and “GDPR appointment reminder” searches without pretending to be a full clinical platform.
  • Trust artefacts: proof policy, AI boundary register, DPA/security-questionnaire preparation checklist and data-minimisation workflow examples.
  • Operational diagnostics: fixed-scope source-to-owner review using buyer-provided exports or screenshots, with no unsupported compliance or outcome claims.
