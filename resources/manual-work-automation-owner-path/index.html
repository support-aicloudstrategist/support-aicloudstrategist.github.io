What the path covers

  1. Name the workflow, trigger and finish line.
  2. Record handoffs, queues and duplicate entry.
  3. Separate stable rules from exceptions needing human review.
  4. Identify the accountable owner and safe evidence boundary.
  5. Choose a reversible next move: clarify, measure, automate or hold.
