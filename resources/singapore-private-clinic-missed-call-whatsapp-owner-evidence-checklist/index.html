What the clinic owner should see every week

1. Source-to-owner queue

Show each missed call, after-hours voicemail, WhatsApp thread, web form, booking request, referral and profile-listing enquiry with a named owner and next action.

2. Appointment follow-up age

Separate same-day reminders, reschedules, cancellations, no-shows, unpaid deposits, report-pickup prompts and recall outreach from generic marketing activity.

3. Human-review stops

Block AI-only replies for symptoms, emergencies, medication questions, identity checks, billing disputes, complaints, data requests and uncertain patient-data handling.

4. PDPA adviser-question lane

Prepare questions about consent, purpose, notification, withdrawal, access/correction, retention, WhatsApp/SMS/email vendors, analytics and cloud storage before automation.

FAQ

Can this work with existing clinic management software?

Yes. It is a no-credentials owner checklist that can sit around existing clinic management software, booking tools, WhatsApp, patient engagement, call tracking and CRM exports before any implementation decision.

Does the CSV contain personal data?

No. The CSV is synthetic and deliberately excludes names, phone numbers, messages, appointment IDs, medical details, NRIC/FIN, payment details and raw WhatsApp exports.
