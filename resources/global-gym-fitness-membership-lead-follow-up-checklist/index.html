Evidence checklist before buying another platform or campaign

1. Source-to-owner lead register

List phone calls, forms, Google Business Profile clicks, Instagram DMs, WhatsApp chats, referral messages, trial bookings, walk-ins, class enquiries and personal-training consultations. Every row needs source, owner, status, age and next action.

2. Missed-call and callback queue

Separate business-hours misses, after-hours calls, unanswered WhatsApp chats, voicemail, call-answering notes and campaign callbacks. Track whether a staff member followed up, not just whether the lead entered a system.

3. Free-trial and consultation no-show log

Track trial booked, attended, rescheduled, no-showed, follow-up sent, objection captured and next owner action. Do not claim conversion improvement; prove the queue first.

4. Membership offer and objection evidence

Capture whether the lead asked about price, location, timings, trainer fit, facilities, family plan, freeze terms, injury concern or personal-training options, then route uncertain or sensitive replies to a human.

5. WhatsApp and consent evidence field

Record what channels are used for reminders and offers, what opt-in/notice evidence exists and which messages should be reviewed by the business owner or adviser before automation.

6. Weekly owner dashboard

Summarize open leads by age, source, class/trial status, next action and owner. The purpose is operational visibility before more ad spend, not a fake revenue forecast.

FAQ

Can a fitness studio use this checklist without changing systems?

Yes. The first step is an evidence map using existing call logs, forms, booking exports, WhatsApp screenshots where appropriate, CRM statuses and owner interviews.

Does AICS replace gym software, CRM or booking platforms?

No. AICS should sit around those tools as the evidence, workflow and owner-dashboard layer when the business needs to prove what is leaking before implementing more automation.
