Minimum evidence sequence before buying

  1. Name the workflow and accountable owner using synthetic or redacted inputs.
  2. Separate patient-data, adviser and production-access questions from operational improvement questions.
  3. Show unknowns and expiry dates rather than filling gaps with assumptions.
  4. Compare existing clinic software, patient-engagement/AI receptionist, cloud/MSP, FinOps and qualified legal/privacy/security advisers against the actual decision.
