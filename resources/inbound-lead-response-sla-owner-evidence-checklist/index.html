7 owner checks before automation spend

  1. Lead source map: list forms, WhatsApp, phone, email, ads, marketplaces and referrals without exporting private messages.
  2. First-response SLA: define target owner response windows by source and business hours.
  3. Queue visibility: mark where enquiries wait: inbox, spreadsheet, CRM stage, call log, booking tool or owner memory.
  4. Handoff stop-rule: define when AI or automation must stop and route to a human.
  5. Lost-reason evidence: use redacted categories only, not customer names or sensitive records.
  6. Claim boundary: do not claim recovered revenue, conversion lift or ROI until verified against real closed-won and source data.
  7. Next safe pilot: choose one source, one owner, one SLA and one proof dashboard before scaling.
