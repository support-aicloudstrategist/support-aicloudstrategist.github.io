FAQ

Can a buyer share patient records for this diagnostic?

No. The first review uses redacted workflow names, owner notes, non-sensitive summaries, public questions and synthetic samples only.

Is this useful before buying another tool?

Yes. It shows whether the issue is follow-up ownership, patient-data boundary, evidence quality, cloud/AI spend accountability, adviser approval or platform capability before spend.

Does AICS guarantee UAE ranking, savings, compliance or patient growth?

No. This package improves evidence quality, positioning and safe scoping; it does not guarantee rankings, savings, compliance, leads, customers, revenue or patient outcomes.
