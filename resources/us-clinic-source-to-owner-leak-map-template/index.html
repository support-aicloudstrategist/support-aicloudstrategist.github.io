4. How a clinic owner or administrator can use it

  1. Inventory every source: phone, missed calls, Google Business Profile, ads, website forms, referral fax/email, scheduling links, portal messages, SMS/email and telehealth.
  2. Mark a single owner or queue for every request, then flag “No owner”, “Blocked”, “Overdue” and “Needs human review”.
  3. Separate operational evidence from PHI. The public/demo version should never include patient names, diagnoses, chart notes, policy numbers or call recordings.
  4. Ask qualified advisers to review HIPAA, privacy, security and clinical escalation requirements before production handling.
  5. Use the result as the evidence base for AICS Patient GrowthOS, not as a replacement claim for AI receptionist or patient engagement vendors.
