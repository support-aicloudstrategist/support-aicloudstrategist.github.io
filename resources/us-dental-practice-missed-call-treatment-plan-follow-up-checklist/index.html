US Dental Practice Missed Call + Treatment Plan Follow-Up Checklist

For dental practices, DSOs and specialty clinics that receive demand through phone, web forms, SMS, WhatsApp, Google Business Profile, referrals and insurance questions but cannot prove which patient enquiries were assigned, followed up, booked, reactivated or left unresolved.

Request a dental follow-up reviewBrowse resources

Use this before buying more ads, AI receptionist software or patient-engagement tools.

1. Capture every entry point

List missed calls, voicemails, web forms, chat, SMS, WhatsApp, referral handoffs, hygiene recall lists, emergency requests, implant/cosmetic consult enquiries and insurance-verification questions.

2. Separate clinical from operational work

Mark what staff can safely triage, what needs dentist/clinical review, what needs billing/insurance ownership and what should never be automated without qualified approval.

3. Build owner-visible queues

Create simple unresolved, waiting-on-patient, waiting-on-practice, booked, no-show, treatment-plan-pending and reactivation queues before changing tools.

Evidence checklist

  • Phone evidence: missed-call and voicemail exports, call-back attempts, after-hours timing and unresolved emergency-intent notes where available.
  • New-patient evidence: form submissions, source, service interest, insurance question status, booked/not-booked state and assigned owner.
  • Treatment-plan evidence: presented plan date, follow-up promise, financing or insurance blocker, next action and human owner.
  • Recall/no-show evidence: hygiene recall lists, appointment reminders, cancellation/no-show states and documented patient opt-out handling.
  • AI boundary evidence: what an AI receptionist or automation may collect, what it may not decide, escalation wording and human-review route.
  • HIPAA/privacy boundary evidence: PHI minimization, approved tool list, access ownership and adviser-reviewed notices where required.

Claim boundaries

This page is a checklist and package entry point, not a client case study, medical recommendation, legal opinion, HIPAA compliance certification, patient-engagement software endorsement, revenue guarantee or booking guarantee. AICS can help map operational evidence and follow-up workflows; qualified dental, legal, privacy, security and compliance advisers must approve regulated decisions.

FAQ

Can AICS replace dental practice management software?

No. AICS should sit around existing PMS, phone, forms, SMS, WhatsApp, CRM-lite or patient-engagement tools to expose leakage and owner responsibility before more automation is added.

Can this guarantee more patients or treatment acceptance?

No. The checklist identifies follow-up gaps and evidence needs. It does not guarantee bookings, revenue, treatment acceptance, rankings or compliance.
