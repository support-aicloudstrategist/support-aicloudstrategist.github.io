What the diagnostic reviews

1. Missed-call and callback map

Capture call-source assumptions, after-hours gaps, voicemail handling, callback ownership, speed-to-lead risk and owner visibility gaps.

2. Appointment handoff controls

Review website form, phone, SMS, email, chat and front-desk handoffs so high-intent implant, Invisalign, emergency and new-patient enquiries do not disappear.

3. AI receptionist boundaries

Define safe prompt boundaries, human escalation triggers, emergency routing, PHI-sensitive intake cautions and no medical-advice guardrails.
