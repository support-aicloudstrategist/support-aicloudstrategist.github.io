The 10 evidence checks before scaling renewal automation

1. Renewal calendar ownership

Map contracts by renewal window, owner, customer-side sponsor, risk state, next action and escalation path without exposing company names or contract values.

2. Product-use evidence boundary

Review safe summaries of login, workflow, integration and adoption signals; do not export raw user-level analytics by default.

3. Support and implementation ageing

Separate open bugs, delayed integrations, onboarding gaps, data migration blockers and training requests from generic health scores.

4. Executive sponsor gap

Flag accounts with no current business sponsor, no recent executive touch, unclear outcome owner or unresolved procurement owner.

5. Security and vendor-risk renewal blockers

Make DPA, subprocessor, AI-use, SOC 2, SSO, invoice, procurement and legal blockers visible as owner queues, not buried email threads.

6. Expansion readiness

Separate real expansion intent from feature requests, support debt, pricing exceptions and customer-side dependency gaps.

7. Save-risk human handoff

Define when CS, sales, support, product, finance, legal or founders must take over instead of sending another automated sequence.

8. Redacted evidence pack

Use anonymised account segments, issue classes and owner states for review; remove names, emails, contracts, tickets, invoices and revenue data by default.

9. Claim approval gate

Hold public or sales claims about retention lift, churn reduction, renewal saved, expansion, ROI and customer outcomes unless approved owners have measured proof.

10. Tool-neutral backlog

Rank fixes across current CRM, CS platform, support desk, analytics, calendar, spreadsheet and BI workflows before recommending a replacement tool.
