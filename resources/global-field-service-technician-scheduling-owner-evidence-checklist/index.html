AICS wedge

Many teams jump from manual dispatch pain straight to FSM software or AI scheduling. AICS first builds the owner evidence layer: request source, customer update, technician assignment, parts blocker, quote status, follow-up owner, escalation trigger and safe automation boundary.

Top-5 owner evidence checks

  1. Request source: phone, WhatsApp, form, marketplace, referral, contract desk, repeat customer or technician-originated task.
  2. Dispatch owner: named accountable person, not just a shared inbox, group chat or spreadsheet row.
  3. Technician status: assigned, waiting for confirmation, en route, blocked, rescheduled, completed or needs quote/follow-up.
  4. Parts/access blocker: missing part, site access, customer availability, warranty approval, payment, safety gate or manager decision.
  5. Customer update and revenue gate: last update sent, next owner, quote status, abandoned job, revisit needed or stop-before-automation trigger.

5-day diagnostic package

Days 1-2: leakage map

  • Map 3-5 request sources and handoffs from lead/request to dispatch, customer update, technician visit and quote follow-up.
  • Separate missed-call, reschedule, parts, access, quote and owner-decision blockers.
  • Identify what can be reviewed without customer records, credentials or production exports.

Days 3-5: owner board

  • Create an owner evidence board with request source, dispatch owner, technician state, blocker, customer update and next action.
  • Define stop-before-automation gates for payments, safety, access, regulated work, warranties and customer complaints.
  • Deliver a shortlist recommendation: process fix, CRM/FSM cleanup, call handling, WhatsApp automation, AI assist or no-tool change.
