Top-5 consideration checklist for UK private clinics

  1. Demand-source map: Can you list phone, web form, email, ads, Google Business Profile, Doctify-style profile, referrals, online booking, WhatsApp/SMS and CRM sources in one owner view?
  2. Missed-call queue: Can reception and management prove same-day callbacks, after-hours follow-up, unresolved enquiries and staff ownership?
  3. Booking handoff evidence: Can you show what happened between enquiry, triage, appointment offered, cancellation, no-show and reactivation?
  4. UK GDPR-aware operating questions: Are consent wording, sensitive-health-data minimisation, retention, processor handoff and access boundaries at least visible for adviser review?
  5. Automation boundary: Is AI or workflow automation limited to safe routing, reminders, owner dashboards and draft assistance rather than unsupervised clinical, legal or regulated decisions?

FAQ

Should a UK private clinic buy software first or map leakage first?

If there is no shared evidence of missed calls, profile enquiries, callbacks and booking handoffs, a short diagnostic usually reduces software-selection risk. It clarifies what the new or existing tool must prove.

Can AICS work with existing PMS, booking and CRM tools?

Yes, the diagnostic is designed to sit around existing systems by mapping source ownership, unresolved queues, workflow status and owner reporting. Access and privacy boundaries must be agreed before any implementation.

Does this asset make ranking claims?

No. It is intended to improve discoverability coverage and buyer consideration language, but it does not claim current or future search rankings.
