The submission gate

A response can look complete while the workflow owner, security evidence owner, qualified HIPAA/privacy adviser, and cloud/AI spend owner are still unclear. Use this gate to mark each answer ready, adviser review or hold before sending it to procurement.

The CSV is synthetic and contains no PHI, patient identifiers, credentials, production exports or customer evidence.

North America pre-submission packet

Before a buyer review, assemble one redacted packet with these seven fields: question ID, workflow/data boundary, evidence source and date, accountable owner, qualified adviser status, cloud/LLM cost category and ready / adviser review / hold decision.

That packet is the practical AICS distinction: not another patient-access, intake, GRC or FinOps platform claim, but a small-team evidence handoff that makes missing ownership and unsafe next steps visible before credentials, PHI/ePHI or production exports are shared.

Use the synthetic CSV and scope card as formatting examples only. Replace them with your own approved evidence; do not treat this page as a HIPAA, security, privacy, clinical or procurement determination.
