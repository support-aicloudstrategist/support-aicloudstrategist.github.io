Top Azure cost questions owners should answer first

1. Who owns the spend?

Map subscriptions, resource groups, tags or service families to a business owner before cancelling, rightsizing or reserving anything.

2. What business purpose does it serve?

Label each line as production, staging, backup, analytics, AI/API workflow, customer delivery, compliance retention or unknown.

3. What can be reviewed without credentials?

Start with redacted service-family totals, masked screenshots or exported summary rows; avoid tenant access, secrets, keys or customer data by default.

4. What must not be touched yet?

Stop production, retention, security, privacy, region and customer-impact decisions until the accountable technical or adviser owner approves.

5. What is a safe action?

Separate quick owner decisions from engineering changes, procurement renegotiation, reserved-capacity decisions, adviser review and blocked items.

6. What proof is needed before a savings claim?

Require approved action dates, baseline windows, post-action bill periods and finance-owner review before publishing savings, ROI or runway claims.
