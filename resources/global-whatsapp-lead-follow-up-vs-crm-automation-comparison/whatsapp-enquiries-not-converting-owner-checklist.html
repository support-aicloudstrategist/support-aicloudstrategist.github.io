WhatsApp enquiries not converting? Start with the owner queue.

If enquiries arrive but follow-up is unclear, the first question is usually not which chatbot to buy. It is where the handoff, ageing, consent or closure evidence disappears.

Five checks before more automation

  1. Source: Can the owner see whether the enquiry came from WhatsApp, call, form, social, referral or marketplace?
  2. Response: Is first response time recorded without treating a sent message as a completed outcome?
  3. Handoff: Is one person accountable for the next step, including evenings and absence cover?
  4. Ageing: Can the team find new, same-day, 1–2 day and older unresolved enquiries?
  5. Boundary: Are consent, sensitive data, pricing and regulated questions routed to a human?

Smallest safe test

Use a redacted or synthetic queue for one workflow. Define the owner, exception path, stop condition and review date before connecting credentials, customer exports or automated messages.

Decision rule: if the owner or exception path is missing, hold tool selection and fix the evidence gap first.

What this helps a buyer decide

This checklist helps distinguish a visibility problem from a tooling problem. A CRM, WhatsApp provider, chatbot, agency or spreadsheet may each fit different conditions; none automatically proves conversion, revenue or safe operation.
