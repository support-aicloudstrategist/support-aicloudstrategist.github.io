Why buyers get stuck

WhatsApp, calls, Instagram DMs, website forms, Google Business Profile clicks, spreadsheets and CRM tasks often show activity but not accountable closure. Owners need to see which leads are ageing, who owns the next action and whether a human review is needed before adding more automation.

Top-3 / top-5 consideration angle

Tool-neutral comparison map

What CRMs and chatbots are good for

  • Capturing structured fields when the team uses them consistently.
  • Assigning tasks, reminders and status changes.
  • Triggering approved messages after opt-in and policy checks.
  • Reporting pipeline stages when source data is clean.

What AICS reviews first

  • Lead source-to-owner map across WhatsApp, calls, forms and DMs.
  • Ageing queue by missed callback, quote pending and decision blocker.
  • Consent, opt-out, sensitive-data and unsupported-claim boundaries.
  • Owner dashboard fields before software migration or automation spend.

Evidence fields small businesses should collect

  • Lead source: WhatsApp, phone, Instagram, website form, Google profile, referral, ad, marketplace or repeat customer.
  • Need and value band: appointment, quote, demo, consultation, purchase, renewal, support or complaint.
  • Status: new, contacted, waiting on customer, quote sent, payment pending, booked, no-show, closed won, closed lost, duplicate or no-consent.
  • Owner and next action: salesperson, receptionist, founder, support, finance, operations, technician or human specialist.
  • Ageing: under 1 hour, same day, 1-2 days, 3-7 days or older than 7 days.
  • Safety boundary: price commitment, medical/legal/financial advice, refund dispute, regulated product, identity issue or sensitive personal data.
