AI workflow: auto-act or ask first?

Use five checks to decide whether a proposed AI step has a clear enough boundary to proceed automatically—or should pause for an authorised human.

The five checks

  1. Impact: could the action affect money, access, safety, privacy or a customer commitment?
  2. Rule: is the permitted action explicit, testable and current?
  3. Evidence: can the workflow show the input, decision and output for review?
  4. Owner: is an authorised person accountable for exceptions and rollback?
  5. Hold: if any check is unclear, does the workflow stop and ask first?

Boundary: local policy and authorised human owners always override this educational aid. It is not a security assessment, legal or compliance advice, production authorisation, performance guarantee, client case study or customer result.
