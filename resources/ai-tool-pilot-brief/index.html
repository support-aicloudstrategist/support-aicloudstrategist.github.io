Before you buy an AI tool, make the pilot measurable.

Use this six-box brief to define the job, accountable owner, baseline, safety boundary, review gate and stop rule before spend, credentials or production access.

The six boxes

  1. Job: name the workflow and the decision it supports.
  2. Owner: identify who is accountable for the pilot and its outcome.
  3. Baseline: record the current process and a measurable starting point.
  4. Safety boundary: define data, access, human-review and rollback limits.
  5. Review gate: set the evidence and decision date before expansion.
  6. Stop rule: state what ends, pauses or escalates the pilot.

Turn the brief into a scoped decision
