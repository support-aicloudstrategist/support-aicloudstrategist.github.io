01 · Executive summary

What finance and engineering should see first

Month-over-month spend rose about 14%. Most of the increase concentrates in compute and data transfer; marketplace and support lines are flat. Ownership mapping shows three patterns: checkout compute is assigned and actionable; the shared EKS platform is contested; GPU batch capacity lacks a clear idle policy.

  • Owned: 61% of spend maps to a named product or team.
  • Shared / gap: 27% needs a split rule or owner before banking cuts.
  • Near-term acts: idle non-prod and orphan storage only where confidence is high.

Recommended posture for the next 30 days: act on clear idle waste, investigate shared-platform allocation, and hold on GPU/production cuts until utilization evidence and owners exist.

03 · Top cost drivers

What moved—and why it matters

  1. 01
    Checkout compute grew with traffic and stale ASG floors.

    Product owns the pool. Utilization evidence supports rightsizing non-peak capacity without touching latency SLOs.

  2. 02
    Shared EKS platform has no product split rule.

    Platform and three product teams disagree on ingress and logging attribution. Cuts here are not bankable until ownership is explicit.

  3. 03
    GPU batch runs nights with weak idle thresholds.

    ML ops owns jobs, not the idle window policy. Hold production GPU cuts; investigate weekend idle separately.

  4. 04
    Orphan snapshots and log retention outlived the feature.

    Storage lines map to a retired experiment. High confidence for cleanup after a short owner confirm.

06 · 30-day action plan

What the client owns next

  1. Week 1

    Confirm owners

    Lock product vs platform ownership for checkout and log retention. Name the EKS split rule owner.

  2. Week 2

    Execute high-confidence acts

    ASG floor change and orphan cleanup with a rollback note. No production GPU changes.

  3. Week 3

    Investigate shared platform

    Propose namespace/ingress allocation rule. Keep contested dollars out of finance forecasts.

  4. Week 4

    Hold review

    GPU idle policy workshop. Hold remains first-class until utilization evidence is trusted.
