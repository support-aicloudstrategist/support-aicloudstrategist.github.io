Evidence fields to collect without patient data

  • Demand source counts by week and channel.
  • Response SLA bands, not names or phone numbers.
  • Booking-handoff reason codes and owner roles.
  • Adviser questions for GDPR, retention, vendor access and DPIA triggers.
  • Cloud or AI cost owner notes without credentials, invoices, logs or architecture secrets.
  • Human review and stop rules before any AI replies to patients.

FAQ

Why not just contact clinics?

Raj explicitly corrected the strategy: AICS should build proof, differentiated value and discoverability first. This asset improves the page that the internal benchmark flagged as a top repair item before buyer reuse.

Can this be used for regions outside Europe?

Yes, only as a structure. Local privacy, healthcare, advertising and procurement rules must be reviewed with qualified advisers before any patient-data processing or public compliance claim.

What is the first commercial step?

A no-patient-data fit check or a fixed-scope diagnostic that maps sources, SLAs, booking handoffs, AI stop rules, trust evidence and owner decisions before platform spend.
