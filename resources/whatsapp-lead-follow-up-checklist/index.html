The nine checks before buying another WhatsApp tool

1. Enquiry source

Capture whether the chat came from website, ad, referral, Google Business Profile, Instagram, QR code, repeat customer or staff-shared number.

2. First-response SLA

Define what counts as an on-time response by source, service urgency, business hours and after-hours handoff instead of trusting unread chat counts.

3. Staff owner

Every lead needs a named owner, backup owner, next action and due time so responsibility is not hidden in a shared phone.

4. Qualification fields

Record service need, location, budget range, timeline, callback preference and blocker questions in a structured CRM or sheet.

5. Quote follow-up

Track quote sent date, quote amount band, next reminder, decision reason and stale quotes separately from new enquiries.

6. Consent and opt-out

Check appropriate consent wording, purpose limits, opt-out handling and local privacy/platform-policy review before automating repeat messages.

7. Chatbot handoff

Route price objections, complaints, urgent requests, identity/payment issues, legal/privacy questions and unclear intent to a qualified human.

8. Closure reason

Use simple reasons such as no response, not fit, price, timing, competitor, duplicate, booked, purchased, refund issue or needs owner review.

9. Owner dashboard

Show new, contacted, qualified, quoted, follow-up overdue, won, lost and unresolved counts by source and owner.

Yes as an operational checklist, but messaging, privacy, consent, advertising and platform rules vary by country and need qualified local review.
