FAQ

Does AICS provide Singapore PDPA legal advice?

No. AICS provides operational workflow mapping, evidence ownership and implementation support. Legal interpretation, DPO decisions and regulatory advice should come from qualified Singapore counsel or privacy professionals.

Does this guarantee PDPA compliance?

No. The package does not certify compliance, guarantee PDPC outcomes, provide audit attestation or promise security, revenue, booking, ranking or AI accuracy results.

What should be ready before starting?

Bring examples of forms, WhatsApp scripts, CRM fields, booking tools, vendor/cloud systems, privacy notices, request-handling steps and planned AI automations. Synthetic/demo evidence can be used if production access is not appropriate.
