The ten checks before buying more dental leads or automation

1. Missed-call capture

Separate answered calls, missed calls, callback requests, clinic-hours calls, after-hours calls and location-specific calls instead of relying only on call logs.

2. WhatsApp opt-in evidence

Record whether the patient asked for WhatsApp communication, what was sent, who replied, what consent or notice evidence exists and what should not be automated.

3. First-callback SLA

Define what must be called back within 15 minutes, 2 hours, same day and next business day; ageing queues reveal whether receptionist capacity is the bottleneck.

4. Source-to-chair tracking

Track Google Business Profile, Practo-style listing, website form, paid ad, referral, walk-in, repeat patient and WhatsApp source through appointment and treatment-plan stage.

5. Treatment-plan follow-up

Separate enquiry, consultation booked, consultation attended, treatment estimate shared, decision pending, finance question, dentist review needed and closed-lost reasons.

6. No-show recovery

Map reminders, reschedule attempts, wait-list fills and no-show reasons before buying more ads that may leak through the same front-desk process.

7. Dentist review boundary

Route pain, swelling, medication, diagnosis, implant/orthodontic suitability, procedure risk and complaint questions to qualified dental staff; do not let AI improvise.

8. Offer and claim review

Keep whitening, implant, aligner and cosmetic offer messages reviewable so marketing claims, discounts and before/after communication stay within clinic-approved boundaries.

9. Owner dashboard

Give the clinic owner a daily view by source, staff owner, callback SLA, missed calls, WhatsApp pending, booked, attended, treatment pending and unresolved reason.

10. Proof-before-platform backlog

List workflow, template, routing, dashboard and measurement fixes before replacing the phone system, clinic software, CRM, WhatsApp provider or marketing agency.
