The 10 evidence checks before cutting cloud or AI spend

1. Owner for every large line item

Use service family, project, tag or masked invoice rows to identify a named business owner before any cancel, shutdown or rightsizing decision.

2. Business purpose before action

Mark each item as production, staging, experiment, backup, analytics, campaign, AI/API workflow, compliance retention or unknown.

3. No-credentials first review

Start with redacted exports, screenshots or synthetic rows; do not request cloud-console access, secrets, API keys or production data by default.

4. AI and API spend source

Map model/API family, feature owner, usage band and approval trail without prompts, customer content or private keys.

5. Retention and backup owner

Separate stale snapshots/logs from legal, privacy, security, restore and customer-contract questions requiring qualified review.

6. Data transfer and region drift

Flag cross-region or duplicated environment spend while avoiding unsupported data-residency, security or compliance claims.

7. SaaS renewal date

Track renewal, seat count, workflow owner and usage evidence before cancellation or negotiation.

8. Rollback and customer-risk gate

Do not shut down technical assets until an accountable technical owner approves rollback, monitoring and customer-impact boundaries.

9. Savings-claim approval

Block public savings, ROI or runway claims until an approved action has a measured post-action billing period and finance owner review.

10. Monthly owner review

Convert the diagnostic into an owner queue: keep, reduce, schedule, rightsize, terminate, negotiate or adviser-review.
