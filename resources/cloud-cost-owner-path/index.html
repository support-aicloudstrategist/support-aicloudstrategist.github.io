Common questions

What should I do when cloud cost is out of control?

Confirm the time window and scope, separate assigned from shared or unallocated spend, name an accountable owner, and choose act, investigate or hold before making a risky cut.

Should we cut the biggest cloud cost first?

Not automatically. Check whether the line is correctly scoped, owned, reversible and connected to reliability or commercial commitments; hold action when evidence is incomplete.
