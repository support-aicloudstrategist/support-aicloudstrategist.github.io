US Specialty Clinic AI Receptionist No-PHI Scope Memo

Use this memo when a clinic is comparing an AI receptionist for medical practice, HIPAA AI receptionist, healthcare voice agent, patient engagement platform, EHR/PMS module or medical office call answering service and needs a safe first review before PHI, credentials, BAA assumptions or ROI claims.

Truth boundary: this is a synthetic buyer-education and scoping memo. It is not legal, privacy, security, clinical, billing or procurement advice. It does not claim AICS has a US specialty clinic customer, signed BAA, HIPAA/SOC 2/HITRUST certification, patient outcome, appointment growth, no-show reduction, revenue, ROI, ranking, platform partnership or AI accuracy result.

Forwardable memo block

Decision to make: whether we should shortlist, demo or buy an AI receptionist / patient-access option for missed patient calls, front-desk backlog, scheduling requests, referral intake or after-hours coverage.

Allowed in first review: public vendor pages, blank workflows, redacted screenshots, role-level process descriptions, approved scripts, no-PHI call categories, owner-defined scoring criteria and unanswered question logs.

Not allowed in first review: PHI/ePHI, patient lists, appointment exports, call recordings, EHR/PMS credentials, production API keys, login sharing, signed BAA assumptions, unreviewed clinical routing, revenue claims or ROI promises.

Required adviser lanes: HIPAA/privacy, security, legal, clinical operations, billing, procurement and IT integration owners must approve regulated or production decisions before any implementation.

Evidence AICS can produce without PHI

  • Vendor evidence request list for BAA, subprocessor, retention, recording and model-training boundaries.
  • No-PHI route map showing where missed calls, callbacks, referrals and scheduling blockers become owner-visible tasks.
  • Claim-control checklist that separates public promises from verified clinic evidence.
  • Owner dashboard spec for unresolved queue age, stop rules, open adviser questions and next decision.
  • Buyer-sendable shortlist memo that names gaps without ranking vendors or claiming superiority.

Stop rules before demos or integration

  1. No patient data, credentials, call recordings or EHR/PMS exports are shared during the first review.
  2. No compliance, BAA, appointment-growth, revenue, ROI or AI-accuracy claim is accepted without source evidence and adviser review.
  3. No autonomous handling is scoped for urgent, clinical, identity mismatch, billing dispute, complaint or consent-sensitive cases.
  4. No production vendor access is granted until owner, security and procurement lanes are named.
