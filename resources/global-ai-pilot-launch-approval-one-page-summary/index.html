Why it matters

Many AI demos reach leadership with scattered screenshots, vendor promises and optimistic outcomes, but without one owner-approved page showing what is proven, what is not proven and what launch boundaries protect the business.

AICS use

AICS uses this summary to make AI pilot evidence board-ready without exaggerating savings, accuracy, security, compliance or customer impact.

One-page executive summary structure

Recommended decision: Go / Restricted go / Redesign / Pause / No-go

1. Decision ask
What leadership is being asked to approve, for which workflow, users, data and region.
2. Evidence confidence
What the pilot actually measured, baseline quality, sample coverage, failure examples and exclusions.
3. Risk acceptance
Data, security, model quality, human-review, legal/compliance adviser questions and unresolved assumptions.
4. Cost boundary
Token/API/cloud budget, scale assumption, alert threshold and owner with authority to pause spend.
5. Operating controls
Runbook, rollback path, change gate, incident route, review cadence and human escalation point.
6. Safe claim boundary
Approved internal/external wording and claims that must not be made without more evidence.

Truth boundary

This is a readiness and executive-decision template. It is not a real client case study, testimonial, ranking, certification, legal/security/compliance/procurement/accounting advice, ROI proof, savings proof, search-ranking evidence, production-success proof and not a guarantee that any AI pilot will reduce cost, increase revenue, improve customer outcomes, meet compliance obligations or succeed in production. No outreach was sent.

FAQ

When should this one-page summary be created?
After evidence has been gathered and before leadership approval, board reporting, customer-facing launch, paid rollout or procurement expansion.
Who signs it?
At minimum: business sponsor, product/AI owner, technology/platform owner, risk/security/data owner, operations owner and finance owner.
Where does it fit in the AICS evidence chain?
It summarizes the evidence room, scorecard and decision record into one leadership page that is easier to review, challenge and archive.
