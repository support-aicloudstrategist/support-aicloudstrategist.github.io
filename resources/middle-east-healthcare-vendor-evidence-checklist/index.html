Five evidence requests

What a credible shortlist should make reviewable

1. Workflow scope

Ask the vendor to name the exact enquiry, booking or follow-up task, supported channels, human handoff and out-of-scope cases.

2. Data path

Request a plain-language map of inputs, storage, access, retention, subprocessors and deletion or export handling. Do not send live patient data to test the claim.

3. Evidence owner

Record who owns each statement, its scope and date, and which unresolved question remains with your legal, privacy, security or clinical adviser.

4. Operating control

Confirm reviewer, approval threshold, exception queue, pause mechanism and escalation route before any production change.

5. Cost accountability

Ask who owns usage, cloud/API spend, alerts and budget decisions. Require an observable baseline before treating savings language as evidence.

Shortlist decision

Choose Proceed, Investigate or Hold for each claim. A polished demo is not evidence of fit, safety or compliance.

Category context, not a ranking

Healthcare teams may encounter patient-engagement vendors, AI receptionist or voice-agent vendors, cloud providers and FinOps platforms during research. Those categories are alternatives to evaluate, not endorsements. Compare each option using the same evidence fields in the checklist.

What this does not prove
