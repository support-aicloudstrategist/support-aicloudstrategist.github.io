The 10 evidence checks before scaling SaaS trial automation

1. Signup-source to account-owner map

Separate website, marketplace, partner, webinar, paid-search, sales-led, community and outbound-assisted trial signups, then assign each high-intent account to a named owner.

2. Activation milestone evidence

Track workspace created, first integration, teammate invited, data imported, first report/workflow completed and value event reached without exposing raw customer data.

3. Human-assist trigger

Define when a trial needs sales, support, solution engineering, CS, security, finance or founder follow-up instead of another automated email.

4. Security and procurement blocker queue

Show SOC 2, DPA, subprocessor, AI data-use, SSO, invoice, vendor-risk and legal questions as owner-visible blockers, not hidden email threads.

5. Trial-ageing dashboard

Review stale accounts by segment, activation state, owner, next action, blocker, last touch, buying committee and safe close/lost reason.

6. Expansion and onboarding handoff

Separate trial close from implementation readiness: data migration, integrations, admin access, training, success plan and customer-side owners.

7. Unsafe automation stop list

Escalate legal terms, security claims, compliance promises, pricing exceptions, cancellation threats, sensitive customer data and public ROI claims to accountable humans.

8. Redacted evidence pack

Use anonymised fields and synthetic examples for review; do not export identifiable customer names, emails, contracts, usage logs, support tickets or revenue data by default.

9. Claim approval gate

Hold public claims about conversion lift, activation rates, retention, ROI, pipeline, customer outcomes and security compliance unless approved owners have documented proof.

10. Tool-neutral backlog

Rank fixes across current CRM, product analytics, lifecycle email, helpdesk, calendar, spreadsheet and CS tools before recommending a platform replacement.
