Privacy-aware workflow boundaries

This package is not legal, medical, privacy, security or compliance advice. It creates operational evidence and prompts that a clinic can review with advisers. The copy and workflow avoid unsupported claims about APP compliance, Privacy Act compliance, Health Records Act compliance, NZ Privacy Act compliance, security certification, medical advice, patient outcomes, appointment guarantees, revenue lift or search rankings.

  • Separate appointment/admin requests from sensitive health details.
  • Keep collection purpose, callback ownership and retention prompts visible in the workflow.
  • Escalate urgent, clinical, complaint, access/correction or sensitive health-information requests to the clinic’s approved route.
  • Label any demo, simulated or internal benchmark as demo/simulated/internal.

FAQ

Does this replace HotDoc, Healthengine, Cliniko, Zanda or AutoMed?

No. It is a workflow, handoff and owner-visibility layer around existing booking, patient engagement and practice-management systems.

Is this APP, Privacy Act, Health Records Act or NZ Privacy Act compliance advice?

No. It is operational readiness work and copy hygiene. Clinics should use qualified legal, privacy, medical and security advisers for formal interpretation and compliance decisions.

Can AICS guarantee more appointments?

No. AICS does not guarantee bookings, revenue, no-show reduction, patient outcomes or rankings. The package produces evidence, workflow fixes and owner visibility so the clinic can make better decisions.
