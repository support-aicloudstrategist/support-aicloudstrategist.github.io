France patient leakage diagnostic checklist

Entry-route inventory

List phone, website forms, booking links, ads, Google Business Profile, email, WhatsApp-style messaging, referral sources and teleconsultation requests. Each route needs a status owner.

Response-time and callback proof

Run a small mystery-shop style internal audit. Record timestamp, channel, staff handoff, callback result, unresolved queue and next action. Label the evidence as demo/internal unless it is real approved client work.

RGPD-aware consent questions

Check whether forms and casual messaging collect unnecessary health data. Review access, retention, purpose, deletion and processor questions with qualified French/EU advisers. This page is not legal advice.

Owner-visible operating dashboard

Show daily totals for new enquiries, answered calls, missed calls, booking requests, scheduled appointments, no-response items, cancellation recovery and unresolved follow-up.
