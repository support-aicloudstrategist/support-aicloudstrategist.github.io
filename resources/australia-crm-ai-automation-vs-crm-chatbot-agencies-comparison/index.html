Tool-neutral comparison

CRM cleanup

Useful when fields, pipeline stages and ownership are messy but the team can commit to updating records. Risk: software hygiene alone may not fix missed callbacks or unsafe automation promises.

Chatbot or AI receptionist

Useful for routing common enquiries and after-hours capture. Risk: medical, legal, financial, refund, complaint, identity or sensitive-data situations still need explicit human escalation.

Digital agency

Useful for traffic, landing pages and creative. Risk: more enquiries can amplify leakage if response SLAs, CRM fields and closure reasons are not owned.

AICS diagnostic

Useful before larger spend when the owner needs one practical map of sources, ageing, evidence fields, unsafe claims and the safest next build/no-build decision.

When AICS is a fit

  • Enquiries arrive from more than three channels and no one can see one owner queue.
  • CRM records exist but do not show next action, age, consent, blocker or closure reason.
  • Automated replies might touch price promises, regulated advice, complaints, refunds or sensitive personal information.
  • The business wants a fixed-scope first step before paying for CRM migration, chatbot setup, AI workflow build or an agency retainer.

Buyer-safe first decision

Start by deciding whether the real blocker is data hygiene, response ownership, consent/escalation risk, reporting visibility or channel strategy. Only then choose CRM, chatbot, AI receptionist, agency, spreadsheet discipline, AICS implementation or no-build process cleanup.
