Why this exists before another tool

When family enquiries, hospital discharge referrals, tour requests and funding questions leak, vendors usually frame the answer as CRM, care-management software, call answering, local SEO, paid ads, WhatsApp follow-up or AI reception. AICS's top-3/top-5 consideration wedge is the evidence layer: prove source, consent, owner, age, human-review boundary and closure before buying or automating.

Use this before buying another platform

This comparison helps a UK care home ask practical buyer questions: Which route captures missed calls? Which route proves tour-stage movement? Which route avoids unsafe care, safeguarding, capacity, legal, funding or CQC-compliance claims? Which route gives the owner a weekly queue without requiring resident data first?

Fields a shortlist-worthy review should inspect

  • Source: Google Business Profile call, website form, directory, family email, hospital discharge referral, social-care referral, walk-in or WhatsApp/SMS.
  • Context: residential, nursing, dementia, respite, palliative, urgent discharge, private funding, local authority or continuing healthcare.
  • Owner: receptionist, admissions manager, registered manager, clinical lead, finance, care group owner or adviser.
  • Stage: callback due, brochure sent, funding question, tour offered, tour booked, tour attended, assessment pending, admitted, declined or closed.
  • Safety: medical, safeguarding, complaint, medication, mental capacity, identity, legal/funding and privacy questions routed to qualified human review.
