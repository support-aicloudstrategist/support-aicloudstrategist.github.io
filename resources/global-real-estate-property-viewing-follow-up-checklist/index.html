Evidence checklist before buying another property CRM or answering tool

1. Missed-call register

Track buyer, seller, tenant, landlord and valuation calls by property, source, time, team owner, urgency and callback status rather than only counting total enquiries.

2. Portal lead queue

Separate enquiries from Rightmove, Zoopla, Zillow, Realtor.com, Magicbricks, Property Finder, Bayut, Dubizzle and local portals so stale viewing requests do not hide inside email.

3. Viewing follow-up SLA

Record whether viewing requests received a human-confirmed slot, alternative time, property pack, finance/prequalification prompt or closure reason.

4. WhatsApp and email handoff

Map WhatsApp, SMS, email, Instagram and website forms into one owner queue with explicit consent, source and next-action evidence.

5. Human review boundary

Keep valuation advice, negotiation, tenancy terms, legal wording, deposits, refunds, discrimination/fair-housing risk and safety concerns under licensed or senior review.

6. Weekly owner dashboard

Show new leads, missed calls, booked viewings, stale enquiries, valuation requests, owner handoffs and open next actions before buying more portal ads.

Use this as a proof-before-platform filter

If the principal cannot see which calls, portal leads and viewing requests are still open, a new CRM or AI receptionist may make the agency look modern while leakage continues.

FAQ

Can this be used with an existing property CRM?

Yes. The checklist is designed to organize evidence around existing phone logs, portal emails, CRM exports, WhatsApp conversations, showing calendars and owner handoff notes.
