Clinic missed-call callback FAQ

What should a clinic do first when calls are missed?
Choose one safe time window, reconcile the aggregate call total and assign a callback owner plus backup before changing tools.
Should names, phone numbers or recordings be uploaded for a first review?
No. Start with aggregate counts, role ownership, callback status and review time; hold when a requested input is unsafe or unclear.
Is an AI receptionist automatically the answer?
No. Compare staffing, routing, CRM, reminders and AI options only after the queue, owner and escalation rule are visible.
What proves the process is ready to change?
A dated window, reconciled totals, named accountable role, status vocabulary and an explicit escalation or hold rule.
