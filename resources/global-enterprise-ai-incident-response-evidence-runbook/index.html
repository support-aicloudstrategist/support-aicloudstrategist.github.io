5-step operating runbook

  1. Detect and classify. Capture the monitoring signal, affected agent, affected workflow, severity, data sensitivity and immediate owner.
  2. Contain without over-claiming. Pause risky actions, switch to draft-only mode, disable a tool permission, route to a human queue, or restore the last approved prompt where the authorised owner approves it.
  3. Record rollback and fallback evidence. Document the exact rollback or fallback action, the accountable owner, and how users or customers continue safely while review is open.
  4. Control communications. Use only approved internal or external messages and keep legal, cybersecurity, insurance, clinical, financial or regulatory questions with qualified advisers.
  5. Verify corrective action. Attach regression evidence, owner approval, monitoring checks and closure status before declaring the incident resolved internally.
