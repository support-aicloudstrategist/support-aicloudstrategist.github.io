The ten checks before buying more field-service software, call coverage or AI

1. Request source capture

Separate Google Business Profile calls, paid-call extensions, repeat-customer texts, referral calls, website forms, chat, SMS, review-site leads and after-hours voicemail.

2. Missed and after-hours queues

Make missed calls, abandoned calls, voicemail and answering-service messages visible in one queue with service line, urgency, owner and next-action due time.

3. Named follow-up owner

Every quote request, repair request, maintenance request and complaint needs an accountable dispatcher, office manager, technician lead or owner.

4. Emergency and high-urgency review

Flag no-heat/no-cooling, leaks, electrical faults, lock-out/no-access and safety-sensitive wording for human review without treating this checklist as safety guidance.

5. Quote-vs-job separation

Track estimate requested, quoted, waiting on customer, booked, dispatched, completed, declined, duplicate and unresolved separately.

6. Dispatch handoff visibility

Show whether work is assigned, waiting on customer, waiting on parts, waiting on technician capacity or blocked by address/payment/access information.

7. Platform touched

Log whether the request touched field-service software, CRM, call tracker, answering service, SMS/review tool, spreadsheet, inbox or AI chatbot.

8. AI and answering-service boundaries

Document which urgent, ambiguous, complaint, quote-exception or safety-sensitive messages require human review instead of automated closure.

9. Closure reasons

Standardise booked, completed, customer declined, duplicate, no response, out of service area, waiting on parts, not a fit and unresolved.

10. Weekly owner dashboard

Review unresolved requests, owner gaps, source gaps, after-hours queue, urgent-review queue, dispatch handoff gaps and closure-reason gaps.

FAQ

Should a contractor buy an answering service first?

Not always. If phone logs, answering-service notes, forms, quotes and dispatch states are not tracked with owner and next action, the first step is often an evidence map and owner queue.

Is this only for HVAC?

No. The checklist can frame a review for HVAC, plumbing, electrical, appliance repair, property maintenance and other home-services teams, with safety, legal, licensing and employment decisions escalated to qualified professionals.
