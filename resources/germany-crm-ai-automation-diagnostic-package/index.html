What the diagnostic produces

  1. Enquiry-source inventory: website forms, phone calls, email inboxes, booking links, CRM stages, spreadsheets and manual handoffs.
  2. Response-SLA and ownership map: who owns each enquiry, expected response window, escalation path, unresolved status and handoff evidence.
  3. CRM gap list: missing source attribution, follow-up date, lost reason, booked/not-booked status, next owner, opt-out field and manager review.
  4. AI automation boundary brief: where chatbot or voice-agent triage is safe, where human review is mandatory, and which medical/legal/financial/compliance claims must be avoided.
  5. GDPR-aware and EU AI Act readiness evidence checklist: operational prompts for purpose, consent, opt-out, retention notes, sensitive-data caution and owner review. This is not legal or compliance advice.
  6. Owner dashboard wireframe: daily unresolved queue, response SLA, booked consultations or calls, follow-up due, no-response leads and source leakage.
  7. 30-day backlog: the first practical automation fixes to build before adding new tools or paid traffic.
