Why this exists before another dealership CRM or BDC tool

Dealers compare CRMs, inventory platforms, phone systems, chat tools, marketplace lead providers, BDC services, call tracking and AI receptionists. The top-3/top-5 consideration gap is usually not another vendor list; it is whether leadership can prove which calls, form fills, marketplace leads, test-drive requests, trade-in enquiries and service-to-sales leads are ageing without an accountable owner.

What AICS must publish/build to earn consideration

AICS should be judged on evidence design: lead-source capture, response SLA, salesperson or BDC ownership, finance and trade-in handoffs, appointment/show status, human-review boundaries, and owner-ready dashboards before any automation or AI recommendation.

Evidence fields to capture

  • Lead source: website form, Google Business Profile call, marketplace lead, OEM lead, paid ad, live chat, WhatsApp/SMS, phone-up, service lane, referral, walk-in or repeat customer.
  • Queue type: new vehicle, used vehicle, test drive, trade-in valuation, finance pre-approval, lease renewal, service-to-sales, parts/service upsell, appointment reschedule or complaint/escalation.
  • Vehicle and buying context: model or stock number, new/used, budget range, urgency, location, trade-in state, financing interest, documents requested and preferred channel.
  • Consent and communication proof: channel permission, opt-out state, identity confidence, sensitive finance-data flag, after-hours request, language preference and human-review requirement.
  • Follow-up movement: first response due, contacted, appointment offered, appointment confirmed, showed, no-show, rebooked, finance pending, manager review, lost reason or closed.
  • Owner and ageing: salesperson, BDC rep, sales manager, F&I, service adviser, GM; under 15 minutes, under 1 hour, same day, 1-2 days, 3-7 days, older than 7 days.
  • Closure reason: sold, appointment booked, unreachable, duplicate, wrong inventory, price mismatch, credit/finance blocker, trade-in blocker, competitor chosen, spam or manager escalation.

Dealer principal dashboard rows

  • Internet leads and missed calls by source, inventory type and age.
  • Test-drive requests without confirmed appointment or owner.
  • Marketplace/OEM leads with no response evidence inside SLA.
  • Trade-in and finance leads waiting for manager/F&I handoff.
  • Service-to-sales opportunities not routed to a named owner.
  • Human-review rows for complaints, finance sensitivity, privacy, identity or legal/adviser questions.

Safe AI and BDC boundaries

  • AI may classify queues, summarize ageing, draft staff-reviewed reminders and surface owner-dashboard gaps.
  • AI should not promise vehicle availability, financing approval, credit terms, warranty coverage, legal rights, regulatory compliance or dealership outcomes.
  • Finance, credit, complaints, cancellation/refund, privacy, identity and legal questions need qualified human review.
  • Measure evidence completeness before claiming sales conversion, show-rate, gross profit, revenue or ROI improvement.

Diagnostic outputs AICS can safely provide

  • Internet lead and missed-call leakage map by source, owner and age.
  • Test-drive, trade-in and finance handoff evidence matrix.
  • BDC/sales SLA and manager escalation draft.
  • CRM/phone/chat/marketplace/AI receptionist comparison questions.
  • Owner-ready weekly dealership follow-up dashboard outline.
