The decision risk

AI pilots often move from impressive demo to production pressure without a single record of baseline, exclusions, failure examples, data limits, cost exposure, owner handoff and approval conditions.

AICS use

AICS uses this format to help teams turn scattered pilot evidence into a buyer-safe production decision pack without inventing ROI, customer impact, compliance or security claims.

Allowed decisions

  • Go: launch inside the approved scope with owners and monitoring.
  • Restricted go: launch to limited users, data, region or workflow until gaps close.
  • Redesign: change use case, workflow, model, controls or operating handoff.
  • Pause / no-go: stop production movement until critical evidence exists.

Truth boundary

This is a readiness and decision-control template. It is not a real client case study, testimonial, certification, legal/security/compliance/procurement/accounting advice, ROI proof, search-ranking evidence, production-success proof and not a guarantee that any AI pilot will reduce cost, increase revenue, meet compliance obligations or succeed in production. FAQ

When should this be created?
Before a pilot moves to production, paid rollout, customer-facing use, board reporting or procurement approval.
Can a tool dashboard replace the decision record?
No. Tool dashboards can be important evidence, but the decision record ties technical signals to business ownership, risk acceptance, budget, operating support and safe claims.
Where should buyers start?
Collect evidence in the evidence room, score readiness, then summarize the final decision in this record.
