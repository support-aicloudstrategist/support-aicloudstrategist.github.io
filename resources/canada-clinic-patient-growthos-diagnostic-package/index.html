What the diagnostic includes

1. Enquiry-source inventory

Website forms, phone calls, missed calls, booking links, Google Business Profile, referral pages, ads, social inboxes, secure messages and telemedicine handoffs are mapped into one source/status register.

2. Callback and booking handoff rules

Define callback SLA, urgent escalation, booking-confirmed, booking-pending, duplicate, not-suitable, no-answer and staff-action states without replacing the clinic’s EMR, billing or practice-management stack.

3. Owner dashboard wireframe

Design a clinic-owner view for source, age, next action, accountable person, unresolved reason, consent prompt evidence and secure-message/booking handoff status.

FAQ

Who is the best-fit buyer?

Owner-led Canadian clinics, dental groups, physio/wellness clinics, aesthetics clinics, specialist practices and healthcare marketing teams that already receive patient enquiries but cannot see who responded, which source worked, which patients fell through and what staff should do next.

Can AI answer medical questions?

No. Any AI receptionist or chatbot in this diagnostic is scoped to administrative capture, routing, reminders and escalation boundaries. Medical advice, diagnosis, treatment guidance and clinical triage should remain with qualified clinical professionals and approved clinic processes.

Where can buyers inspect the method?

Review the linked simulated Canada proof asset. It uses synthetic data only and shows the diagnostic logic before any real customer proof exists.
