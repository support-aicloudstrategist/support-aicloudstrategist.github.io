Use this before the review when

Finance sees invoices, not owners

Cloud, LLM, GPU and observability costs exist, but board-ready rows lack named product, engineering, finance and shutdown owners.

AI experiments became recurring spend

Notebook, demo, evaluation, fine-tuning, batch inference or agent workloads are still running without a documented production, research or stop decision.

Tool decisions need evidence

The team is debating CloudZero, Vantage, native cloud dashboards, LLM observability, internal dashboards or consultants before agreeing what evidence is missing.

Claims need proof discipline

Savings, runway, margin, ROI or customer profitability claims should wait until baseline, action, owner and measurement evidence exists.

One-page review agenda

  1. Open with evidence boundaries: sources included, sources missing and advice areas reserved for qualified specialists.
  2. Show the top AI/cloud cost drivers by owner, product, environment and decision status.
  3. Separate immediate hygiene work from architecture, vendor, commitment and platform decisions.
  4. Assign each action a named owner, due date, risk note and next-review date.
  5. Record what cannot yet be claimed because proof is missing.
