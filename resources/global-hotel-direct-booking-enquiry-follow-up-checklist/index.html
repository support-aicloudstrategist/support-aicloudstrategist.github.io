Evidence checklist before buying another platform or campaign

1. Source-to-owner enquiry register

List phone calls, website forms, Google Business Profile clicks, WhatsApp chats, email enquiries, OTA messages, travel-agent notes, group booking requests and repeat-guest messages. Every row needs source, owner, status, age and next action.

2. Missed-call and reservation callback queue

Separate business-hours misses, after-hours calls, unanswered WhatsApp chats, voicemail, front-desk notes and ad-campaign callbacks. Track whether a human followed up, not just whether the enquiry entered a system.

3. Direct booking and OTA handoff log

Track whether each enquiry came from the website, phone, WhatsApp, OTA, metasearch, repeat guest, corporate desk or travel agent, then record whether rate, availability, payment, cancellation, room type or policy questions were closed.

4. Quote, group and event follow-up evidence

Capture quote sent, proposal owner, room block, banquet/event handoff, deposit question, expiry date, follow-up status and next action without claiming more bookings or revenue.

5. Guest-message and privacy boundary

Record which messages involve identity documents, payment details, health/accessibility needs, legal terms, refunds, cancellations or sensitive guest information and should be reviewed by a human before automation.

6. Weekly hotel-owner dashboard

Summarize open enquiries by age, source, room/date, quoted rate, owner, direct/OTA status and next action. The purpose is operational visibility before more ad spend, not a fake revenue forecast.

FAQ

Can a hotel use this checklist without changing systems?

Yes. The first step is an evidence map using existing call logs, website forms, WhatsApp chats where appropriate, email enquiries, OTA messages, booking-engine exports, PMS status reports and owner/front-desk interviews.

Does AICS replace PMS, channel manager or booking-engine software?

No. AICS should sit around those tools as the evidence, workflow and owner-dashboard layer when the hospitality business needs to prove what is leaking before implementing more automation.
