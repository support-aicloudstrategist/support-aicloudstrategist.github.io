Approval questions

  • What exact decision or workflow will this AI system support?
  • What data can it access, retain or expose?
  • Who can stop it, change it or approve expanded usage?
  • What cost trigger would require finance review?
  • What claims are safe to make without implying guaranteed ROI, compliance or accuracy?

Truth boundary

It is not a real client case study, not vendor ranking, not a testimonial, not a certification, not legal/security/compliance/procurement/accounting advice, not ROI proof, not search-ranking evidence and not a guarantee that an AI purchase will reduce cost, increase revenue, meet compliance obligations, be secure or succeed in production. FAQ

Where should this fit in the buying process?
Use it before final approval, renewal expansion or pilot-to-production handoff, especially when an AI vendor touches customer, employee, financial, operational or regulated data.
Does this checklist approve or reject a vendor?
No. It helps accountable owners see what evidence exists, what gaps remain and what review is required before a decision.
What should AICS review first?
Start with the use case, data boundary, vendor evidence, cost exposure and ownership map. Then decide whether a deeper AI procurement evidence review is useful.
