Truth boundary

This is a buyer-education resource hub. It does not claim real customer outcomes, testimonials, rankings, certifications, revenue impact, ROI, safety, compliance or production readiness. It is not legal, privacy, security, clinical, financial, accounting, procurement or regulatory advice.

Recommended decision path

  1. Capture scope, owners and sensitive-data boundaries with the intake questionnaire.
  2. Map pilot risks, missing evidence and accountable owners in the board risk register.
  3. Separate scale, restrict, remediate, pause and adviser-review decisions before launch.
  4. Check human override, incident, rollback, data residency, vendor lock-in and budget exposure.
  5. Use the diagnostic package when executive sponsors need a board-readable evidence pack.
