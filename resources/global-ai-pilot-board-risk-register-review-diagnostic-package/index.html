Truth boundary

This is a buyer-education and revenue-readiness package page. It is not a real customer case study, not a testimonial, not a certification, not a guarantee that an AI pilot is safe, compliant, profitable, accurate, production-ready or board-approved, and not legal, privacy, security, clinical, financial, accounting, procurement or regulatory advice. No outreach was sent and no customer, lead, ranking, revenue or ROI claim is made.

When this package is useful

Board asks need evidence

The sponsor has pilot metrics, demos, vendor notes and user feedback, but the board still needs named owners, stop conditions, residual risks and decision evidence.

Scale pressure is high

Teams want production expansion, procurement sign-off or customer-facing claims before rollback, incident, data, security, cost and human-override evidence is assembled.

Risk ownership is fragmented

Product, engineering, finance, legal, security, operations and vendors each hold pieces of the answer, but there is no shared AI governance risk register.

External claims are tempting

Marketing, sales or investor updates want strong AI outcomes, while proof boundaries, caveats and adviser questions are not yet explicitly reviewed.

Inputs AICS can review

  • AI pilot charter, success criteria, proof-of-value scorecard, go/no-go record or launch approval summary.
  • Risk register, model/prompt evaluation notes, incident log, human override records, remediation decision log and rollback checklist.
  • Vendor due-diligence notes, data/privacy/security questions, procurement blockers, cloud/LLM usage summaries and cost exposure notes.
  • Redacted screenshots, exported tables or written summaries that the buyer is authorized to share. AICS does not need secrets, PHI, personal data or confidential raw customer records for the first review.

Package output format

The expected output is a board-readable evidence pack: a populated risk-register review table, a decision queue, unsupported-claim list, missing-evidence checklist, adviser-question list and next-review cadence. The package is designed to complement the free AI Pilot Board Risk Register Template, not replace accountable business, legal, compliance, security or engineering decisions.
