FAQ

When should a European SaaS team use AICS?

Use AICS when the blocker is messy evidence: AI workflows are real, but the buyer-facing answer library, trust-centre content, owner dashboard and unresolved adviser questions are not yet clean enough for enterprise review.

When should a buyer choose a platform first?

If the core need is continuous control monitoring, formal GRC workflow, compliance automation, questionnaire-response automation, a trust portal or detailed cloud-cost analytics, evaluate a dedicated platform. AICS can still help prepare the underlying evidence and operating ownership.

Can this support top-3/top-5 consideration?

Yes only as a credibility asset: it makes AICS discoverable for practical buyer questions and shows proof discipline. It is not customer traction, a ranking guarantee, or a substitute for approved real customer proof.
