The ten checks before buying more ads, booking software or AI follow-up

1. Source capture

Separate Google Business Profile calls, website booking forms, Instagram DMs, WhatsApp, phone calls, table-booking apps, delivery apps, walk-ins, repeat customers and referrals.

2. Missed and after-hours queue

Show missed calls, unanswered WhatsApp messages, late-night event enquiries, holiday requests and messages that arrived when the team was busy.

3. Named follow-up owner

Every booking, party enquiry, catering lead, complaint, review response and refund handoff needs a named manager, host, owner or operations lead.

4. Booking and no-show states

Track requested, confirmed, deposit pending, changed time, waiting list, no-show, cancelled, seated, duplicate and unresolved instead of a vague open message list.

5. Private-event and catering handoff

Separate high-value birthday, corporate, group, catering and venue-hire enquiries from normal table bookings so they are not buried in daily chat.

6. Delivery-app exception route

Route missing items, late deliveries, refunds, menu issues and bad reviews to an accountable person without treating this page as delivery-platform advice.

7. Consent and message boundary

Record whether WhatsApp/SMS follow-up has a clear customer context and opt-out route; escalate privacy, marketing and platform-policy decisions to qualified review.

8. AI receptionist boundary

Define which complaints, food-allergy mentions, payment/refund issues, abusive messages, sensitive requests and unclear booking changes need human review.

9. Closure reason

Use standard reasons: booked, seated, waitlisted, cancelled, no response, not available, duplicate, delivery issue closed, complaint escalated and unresolved.

10. Weekly owner dashboard

Review source gaps, missed calls, no-shows, event leads, complaint ageing, review handoffs, owner gaps and unresolved follow-up before spending more on demand.
