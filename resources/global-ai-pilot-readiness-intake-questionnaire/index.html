Truth boundary

This is a buyer-education and readiness asset, not a real customer case study, testimonial, audit, certification, safety proof, compliance proof, ROI proof, ranking claim, lead, customer or revenue evidence. It does not provide legal, privacy, security, clinical or financial advice. No outreach was sent.

What the intake captures

Use-case clarity

Business decision, intended users, excluded use cases, human approval gates and success evidence.

Control readiness

Data sources, privacy/security adviser questions, owner handoffs, incident path and rollback triggers.

Claim boundaries

Cost, accuracy, safety, compliance and ROI statements that must stay internal until evidence is approved.

How AICS uses this intake

  1. Convert incomplete answers into a risk-register and owner queue.
  2. Separate scale-ready evidence from adviser-needed legal, privacy, security, clinical or financial questions.
  3. Produce a board-ready decision trail: scale, restrict, remediate or pause.
  4. Keep unsupported customer-facing claims out of website, sales and investor material until evidence is approved.
