Truth boundary

This is a buyer-education and approval-log template, not a real customer case study, testimonial, audit, certification, safety proof, compliance proof, ROI proof, ranking claim, lead, customer or revenue evidence. It does not provide legal, privacy, security, clinical or financial advice. No outreach was sent.

What the log prevents

Unsupported public claims

Stops “safe”, “compliant”, “autonomous”, “production-ready”, “X% savings” or “guaranteed ROI” language from leaving drafts without approved evidence.

Ownerless evidence

Assigns business, product, finance, security, privacy and legal/compliance owners before claims enter decks, websites or outbound material.

Board-review confusion

Separates approved wording, internal-only wording, adviser-needed questions and rejected claims into one decision trail.

How AICS uses this log

  1. Review proposed AI pilot claims before website, sales, investor or board reuse.
  2. Map every claim to evidence owners, source documents and scope boundaries.
  3. Move unsupported language into rejected, internal-only or adviser-needed status.
  4. Produce a clean approval trail for the board risk register and production go/no-go decision.
