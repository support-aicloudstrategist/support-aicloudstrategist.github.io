Leadership review questions

  • What exact business problem did the pilot prove against?
  • What data, model, workflow or user population is excluded from the claim?
  • What failure modes were found and who owns them after launch?
  • What will this cost at 10x usage and who receives anomaly alerts?
  • What must be true before the pilot claim appears in sales, investor, board or customer material?

Truth boundary

This is a buyer-education and readiness artifact. It is not a real client case study, not production workload proof, not a testimonial, not a certification, not legal/security/compliance/procurement/accounting advice, not ROI proof, not search-ranking evidence and not a guarantee that an AI pilot will reduce cost, increase revenue, meet compliance obligations or succeed in production. FAQ

When should this evidence room be created?
Create it after a working pilot and before production approval, procurement escalation, board reporting or customer-facing claims.
How does this differ from the scorecard?
The AI Pilot Proof-of-Value Scorecard quickly identifies green, amber and red readiness lanes. This evidence room is the organized document index that supports the decision.
Does this replace security, legal, procurement or finance review?
No. It prepares clearer evidence for those reviews and makes gaps visible. Specialist owners still decide security, legal, compliance, procurement and finance conclusions.
