Six evidence lanes

What buyers are trying to de-risk

1. Intended use and risk

Describe the use, users, affected people, human oversight, unacceptable-risk questions and system boundary without calling a product “compliant”.

2. GDPR and DPIA inputs

Map purpose, categories, access, retention, processor/subprocessor roles, transfers and the owner who obtains qualified privacy advice.

3. Security questionnaire

Request dated, scoped evidence for controls, incidents, recovery, model/data paths and unresolved exceptions; separate a demo from proof.

4. Procurement response pack

Give each answer an owner, source, version/date, scope and follow-up. Keep “not applicable” and “under review” visible.

5. Cloud and LLM economics

Assign account, product, environment, model/API, driver, allocation confidence and safe next action before optimizing or promising savings.

6. Decision brief

Summarise Act, Investigate and Hold items with evidence links, due dates, stop rules and a named decision owner.

Buyer language to reflect in your evidence

  • “Show me the evidence owner, scope and date.”
  • “Where does personal or health data go, and who can access it?”
  • “What changes if the model, subprocessor, hosting region or retention policy changes?”
  • “Can finance and engineering explain the cost driver and unit of value?”
  • “What is still unknown, and what is the stop rule?”

Common consideration set to research, not an endorsement: Microsoft Cloud for Healthcare, AWS for Health, Google Cloud Healthcare, Apptio Cloudability, Flexera One and VMware Tanzu CloudHealth. Compare scope, evidence, ownership and fit—not logo count.

What makes a shortlist reviewable

Minimum pack: scope, evidence source, owner, date/version, data-path assumption, cost driver, open exception and hold rule.

Ask: What would change the decision if the model, subprocessor, hosting region, retention or pricing changed?

AICS lens: turn “ready” language into Act, Investigate and Hold items that a buyer can forward internally.

Questions buyers ask before the response is sent

What should we prepare first?

Build a scoped evidence register: intended use and risk questions, GDPR/DPIA inputs for qualified review, dated security evidence, procurement answer owners, and cloud or LLM cost drivers. Keep unknown and under-review items visible.

Does this prove GDPR or EU AI Act compliance?

No. This is synthetic, no-personal-data buyer education—not legal, privacy, clinical, security or compliance advice, certification or an approval decision.

What should we request from a vendor?

Request evidence with scope, owner, date or version, source and unresolved exceptions. Separate a demonstration from proof, and record what changes if the model, subprocessor, hosting region or retention policy changes.

How do we review cloud and LLM costs?

Assign account, product, environment, model or API, driver, allocation confidence and a safe next action before optimization. This map is not a savings, ROI or performance result.
