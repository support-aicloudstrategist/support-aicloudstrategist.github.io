What the diagnostic produces

  1. Manual-work inventory: recurring admin tasks, inbox steps, spreadsheet updates, approvals, booking handoffs, document requests and owner reporting routines.
  2. Leakage and delay map: where work waits because no owner, due date, next action, status or escalation rule is visible.
  3. Automation-fit matrix: tasks suitable for templates, reminders, routing, dashboards, CRM fields, Zapier/Make flows or human review.
  4. CRM and evidence gap list: missing source, owner, next step, due date, lost reason, opt-out, status and manager review fields.
  5. UK GDPR and PECR-aware operating prompts: practical data-boundary questions for consent, purpose, opt-out, retention, sensitive-data caution and owner review. This is not legal, privacy, security or compliance advice.
  6. Owner dashboard wireframe: overdue tasks, unresolved enquiries, upcoming follow-ups, waiting-on-customer items and automation candidates.
  7. 30-day backlog: the first practical fixes to build before complex AI assistants or paid traffic.
