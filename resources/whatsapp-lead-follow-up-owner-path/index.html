Five checks before changing tools

1 · Source

Record whether the enquiry came from WhatsApp, phone, form, referral or another channel.

2 · Owner

Name the person accountable for the next safe action and a backup.

3 · Age

Separate new, same-day, ageing and closed items without uploading message content.

4 · Status

Use plain states such as contacted, waiting, quote sent, booked, duplicate or no-consent.

5 · Hold

Pause when consent, sensitive data, pricing commitment or human review is unclear.
