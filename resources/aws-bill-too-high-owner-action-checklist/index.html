Top AWS cost questions owners should answer first

1. Who owns the AWS account or workload?

Map accounts, linked accounts, tags, products or service families to a business owner before terminating, rightsizing, reserving or refactoring anything.

2. What business purpose does the spend serve?

Label each line as production, staging, backup, analytics, AI/API workflow, customer delivery, security retention or unknown.

3. What can be reviewed without credentials?

Start with redacted service-family totals, masked Cost Explorer screenshots or exported summary rows; avoid account access, keys, secrets and customer data by default.

4. What must not be touched yet?

Pause customer-facing, retention, security, privacy, region, backup and incident-response decisions until accountable technical or adviser owners approve.

5. What is a safe first action?

Separate quick owner decisions from engineering changes, procurement commitments, Savings Plans decisions, adviser review and blocked items.

6. What proof is needed before a savings claim?

Require approved action dates, baseline windows, post-action bill periods and finance-owner review before publishing savings, ROI or runway claims.
