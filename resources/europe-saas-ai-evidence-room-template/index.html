Copyable evidence-room template

Turn scattered SaaS AI governance answers into a buyer-ready evidence table.

Use this template when enterprise buyers ask about AI usage, data boundaries, human review, vendors, model outputs, security controls, cloud or AI spend ownership and unresolved compliance questions. It is operational preparation, not legal, privacy, DPO, audit or certification advice.
