Questions clinic owners ask

What does IVF clinic lead leakage mean?

It is a working description for an enquiry whose next owner, status or callback timing is unclear. This map is a process aid, not evidence of lost patients or revenue.

What should a clinic check before buying lead automation?

Check the enquiry source, accountable owner, response status, human-review boundary and a reversible test using synthetic or redacted data.

Can this worksheet be used with patient details?

No. The worksheet is synthetic and should not receive names, phone numbers, treatment details, recordings, health information or credentials.

Does the map prove more IVF appointments?

No. It only improves process-readiness visibility. Appointment, conversion, patient, savings, compliance and revenue outcomes require separate measured evidence.

What this helps a buyer decide

Whether the next step is to clarify ownership, fix a handoff, ask a vendor a bounded question, or hold spend until the evidence is sufficient. AICS can review a public-information fit without requiring patient data or credentials.
