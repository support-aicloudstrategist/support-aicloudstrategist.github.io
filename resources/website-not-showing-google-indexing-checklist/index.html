Problem-led SEO and trust readiness

Website not showing in Google? Build the evidence before buying more traffic.

For founders and operators searching “why is my website not showing in Google after launch”, this owner checklist shows what to verify before more ad spend, SEO retainers, redesign work, AI content production or marketplace listings.

What owners should check first

  • Robots and meta robots allow priority pages to be indexed.
  • Canonical URLs match the public pages buyers should find.
  • Sitemap exists, returns 200, and includes the pages that explain the real buyer problem.
  • Internal links from home, resources and service pages point to the priority page.
  • Title, description and H1 use customer pain language, not only consultant jargon.
  • Trust evidence is visible: boundaries, proof policy, contact, pricing route and owner next step.

What cannot be claimed without telemetry

  • Google indexing status, impressions, clicks or average position.
  • Search demand, traffic growth, leads, revenue, conversion rate or ROI.
  • Independent endorsement, backlink authority or top ranking.
  • Compliance, legal, privacy, security or advertising performance results.

Demo owner map: from “not on Google” to evidence-led next step

This visual is demo-labelled and uses no customer data. It helps owners inspect the workflow before sharing Search Console, analytics, CMS or ad-account access.

1. Crawl and index gate

Confirm the page is not blocked by robots.txt, noindex tags, broken canonicals, redirects, login walls or accidental staging-domain leftovers.

2. Buyer usefulness gate

Confirm the page answers a real search phrase such as “website not showing in Google”, “Google not indexing my site” or “new website no visitors”.

3. Trust and conversion gate

Confirm buyers can see boundaries, proof policy, contact route, fixed-scope next step and what evidence is still unverified.

Proof boundary

This is a synthetic/readiness checklist and demo visual only. It does not prove Google indexing, rankings, impressions, clicks, visitors, leads, customers, revenue, savings, ROI, ad performance, SEO results, legal/privacy/security/compliance status, backlink authority or independent endorsement. No client, customer analytics, Search Console export, CMS access, production log, credential, testimonial or certification is used or claimed.
