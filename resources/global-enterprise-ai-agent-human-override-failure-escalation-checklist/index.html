Where override evidence usually breaks

Trigger clarity

Teams agree that humans can intervene, but cannot show the exact unsafe-output, confidence, data-age, policy or user-impact condition that requires override.

Fallback ownership

Support, operations, product and engineering assume someone else owns manual continuation when the agent is paused.

Evidence capture

Incidents are discussed in chat or tickets without a redacted evidence log that supports retest, rollback and executive acceptance.

External claims

Marketing, sales or customer-success copy says “safe”, “accurate” or “fully automated” before the evidence boundary has been approved.
