Plain-English answer

First check waste before buying anything new: unused servers, old backups, unattached disks, oversized databases, forgotten test environments, poor tagging, and missing budget alerts. Then decide whether automation, reserved capacity, architecture change, or governance is needed.

What AICloudStrategist checks

We review the bill, services, environments, alerts, owners, and business use. The output is a short leakage list: stop now, resize next, automate later, and monitor monthly.

We do not claim guaranteed savings, rankings, patients, or revenue. We start with a practical review, show visible leakage, then propose the smallest useful fix.
