Plain-English answer

Before spending more on ads, check leakage: missed calls, slow WhatsApp replies, no follow-up reminders, unclear treatment pages, weak reviews/trust signals, and no owner dashboard. More enquiries are useless if the clinic loses them after 6 pm or after the first reply.
