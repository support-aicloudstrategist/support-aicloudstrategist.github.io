The ten checks before increasing admission ad spend

1. Enquiry source capture

Record whether each lead came from Google search, Google Business Profile, Meta ads, school fair, referral, walk-in, aggregator, website form, call or WhatsApp message.

2. Counsellor first-response SLA

Define who responds within 5 minutes, 30 minutes, same day and after-hours windows; missed calls and WhatsApp messages should not depend on memory.

3. Course and batch fit

Capture course, class, exam, city, campus, online/offline preference, target batch, start date, language preference and urgency.

4. Parent/student decision stage

Separate new enquiry, fee asked, demo requested, counselling booked, demo attended, scholarship asked, documents pending, payment pending and lost/not-fit.

5. WhatsApp consent and opt-out evidence

Track preferred channel, opt-in source, opt-out handling, guardian/parent contact boundary and messages needing human review before automation.

6. Fee, batch and proof clarity

Give counsellors consistent answers for fees, batch size, faculty, schedule, demo class, refund/cancellation policy and proof that can be truthfully shown.

7. Demo class handoff

Every demo booking needs owner, date, reminder status, attendance status, feedback captured and next counselling step.

8. Stale lead recovery

Route no-answer, not-now, fee-sensitive, competitor-comparing and result-waiting leads into controlled follow-up instead of abandoned spreadsheets.

9. Lost reason discipline

Use simple lost reasons: wrong course, distance, timing, price, parent declined, competitor, no response, already enrolled, low intent or needs owner review.

10. Owner admission dashboard

Show new enquiries, counsellor SLA breaches, demos booked/attended, hot leads, payment-pending leads, stale leads and source quality by week.

FAQ

Should a coaching institute buy more leads first or fix follow-up first?

If counsellors already have missed calls, stale WhatsApp chats, demo no-shows and no clear hot-lead list, fix follow-up first so new ad spend is not wasted.

Is this only for Indian coaching centres?

No. The checklist is written for India search language, but the same owner-dashboard logic applies to schools, skill academies, tuition centres and online/offline education businesses in other regions with local legal/privacy review.
