Plain-English answer

Admissions leakage usually happens in three places: weak trust before enquiry, slow counsellor follow-up after enquiry, and no clear owner view of pending/hot leads. Before increasing ad spend, an institute should fix landing pages, counselling scripts, WhatsApp follow-up, reminders and a simple admission dashboard.
