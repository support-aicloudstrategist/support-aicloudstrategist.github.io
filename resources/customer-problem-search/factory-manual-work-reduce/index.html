Top leakage symptoms

  • Order status lives in different Excel sheets, notebooks, calls and WhatsApp groups.
  • The owner, manager or coordinator must manually ask for every production-stage update.
  • Dispatch gets delayed because quality check, packing, transport or invoice handoff is not visible.
  • Staff updates are repeated, but not converted into a reliable daily report.
  • Customer commitments are made without seeing bottlenecks, pending materials or capacity conflict.
  • Payment, purchase and dispatch follow-up depends on one person remembering everything.

10-point factory manual-work reduction checklist

  1. Order intake: capture customer name, product/SKU, promised date, quantity, priority and payment terms in one place.
  2. Stage map: define the 5–8 production stages that matter to the owner, not every micro-step.
  3. Owner field: assign a single accountable person for each order or batch.
  4. Material dependency: mark which orders are waiting for raw material, purchase approval or vendor delivery.
  5. Delay reason: require a simple reason when a task crosses the expected date: material, machine, quality, labour, customer approval, transport or payment.
  6. WhatsApp capture: convert repeated WhatsApp updates into structured status, not screenshots buried in groups.
  7. Dispatch handoff: track packing, invoice, e-way/shipping paperwork where relevant, transport booking and customer update.
  8. Payment reminder: connect dispatch/completion status to payment follow-up without exposing sensitive finance data unnecessarily.
  9. Daily dashboard: show pending today, delayed, ready-to-dispatch, awaiting customer, awaiting vendor and owner-attention items.
  10. Automation-first boundary: automate reminders and visibility first; add ERP/MRP only after the real workflow and owner reporting needs are clear.
