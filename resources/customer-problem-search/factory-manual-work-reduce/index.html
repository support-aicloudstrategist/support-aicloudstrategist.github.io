Plain-English answer

Most small factories do not need a heavy ERP on day one. They first need visibility: order status, production stages, dispatch follow-up, purchase/payment reminders and owner dashboard. Reducing manual work starts by mapping repeated WhatsApp/Excel updates and converting the highest-leakage steps into a simple workflow.
