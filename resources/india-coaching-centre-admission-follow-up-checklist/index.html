Buyer problem this page targets

Coaching centre admission enquiries not converting is often not only a marketing problem. The leakage usually sits between missed calls, WhatsApp response delays, trial-class booking, counsellor ownership, fee-plan questions, parent objections and centre-owner visibility.

What to inspect before buying tools

  • How many Google Business Profile, website, call, WhatsApp, Instagram and referral enquiries arrive each day?
  • Which leads get a callback within two working hours and which disappear into counsellor phones?
  • Which demo-class requests are confirmed, attended, rescheduled or lost with no reason?
  • Which parent objections—fees, batch timing, teacher fit, distance, online/offline preference—are logged?
  • What owner dashboard shows source, counsellor, batch, exam/course and next action?

Admission follow-up evidence map

Leakage pointEvidence to collectSafe AI / automation boundary
Missed call or late callbackCall source, time, course interest, callback owner and callback status.AI may summarise call logs or prepare callback queues; humans handle counselling and commitments.
WhatsApp enquiry not progressingFirst response time, course link sent, fee query, location/batch query and next follow-up.AI output is draft support, not an official representation to parents or students.
Demo class booked but not attendedConfirmation message, reminder, attendance, reschedule reason and counsellor action.Automation can remind and route; staff decide batch fit and academic guidance.
Fee or scholarship objectionObjection type, approved fee-plan range, manager approval and final status.No automated fee promise, scholarship promise, guarantee or discount without authorised human approval.
Owner cannot see lost leadsDaily source summary, counsellor pending list, batch-wise admissions pipeline and stale leads.Dashboard supports management review; it does not claim admissions, rank, exam-score or revenue improvement.

Minimum dashboard fields

Lead source, course/exam, branch, counsellor, student/parent route, consent status, callback SLA, demo status, objection, next action, stale-lead age and closure reason.

Comparison lens

Before selecting a CRM, education ERP, WhatsApp automation tool, call centre, ad agency or AI chatbot, check whether it proves counsellor ownership and parent follow-up evidence.

Safe owner action

Start with an evidence audit of 50–100 recent enquiries, not a broad automation rollout. Fix routing, proof, response times and review rules first.
