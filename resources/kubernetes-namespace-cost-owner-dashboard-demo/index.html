Dashboard fields buyers should expect

  • Cluster, namespace, environment and business owner.
  • Monthly spend band and cost trend, without secret account IDs.
  • Requested vs observed CPU/memory or GPU signal.
  • Recommended action: investigate, rightsize, pause, delete, tag, commit or defer.
  • Production-risk note and approval owner before change.
  • Next-review date and unresolved blocker reason.
