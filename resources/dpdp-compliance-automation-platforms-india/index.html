DPDP operating questions to answer before selecting software

Collection inventory

Which website forms, landing pages, WhatsApp flows, CRM fields, booking tools, payment flows, analytics tags and cloud stores collect personal data?

Consent and notice versioning

Can the business prove which notice and purpose language was shown when data was collected, and how withdrawal or correction requests are routed?

Data principal request ownership

Who receives, validates, tracks and closes access, correction, erasure and grievance requests without losing the evidence trail?

Processor and breach evidence

Which vendors process data, what cloud systems store it, who owns incidents and what timestamps, approvals and communications are retained?

FAQ

Does AICS replace DPDP Comply, ComplyMode, ProtectComply, OneTrust, Fortra or Digio?

No. AICS is positioned as an operating-readiness, workflow mapping and implementation support layer. Dedicated DPDP platforms, privacy suites, DPO services and counsel may still be needed.

Is this page legal advice?

No. This is operational guidance for searchers comparing DPDP automation options. AICS does not provide legal advice, certification or a claim that a business is compliant with the DPDP Act.

Where should a small Indian business start?

Start by listing every personal-data collection point, every processor, every consent/notice location, every owner for data principal requests and every evidence record currently available. Then choose software or advisory support based on the gaps.
