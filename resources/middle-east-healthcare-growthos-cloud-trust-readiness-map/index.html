Five evidence lanes

Turn a broad vendor conversation into reviewable ownership.

1. Patient-enquiry flow

Record the enquiry source, callback owner, handoff, response target and exception path without exporting patient data.

2. AI vendor questionnaire

Ask for evidence owner, version/date, scope, data path, subprocessors and unresolved questions before a demo becomes a commitment.

3. Data-responsibility questions

Separate operational questions from advice: who decides purpose, access, retention, incident escalation and review sign-off?

4. Cloud-cost ownership

Map account, cost pool, service owner, driver, confidence and safe next action before changing production settings.

5. Executive decision brief

Summarise Act, Investigate and Hold items with owner, due date, evidence link and stop rule.

Buyer questions before a shortlist

What should we ask an AI vendor?

Ask for workflow scope, data path, evidence owner, human review and pause mechanism. Keep the request no-PHI until the relevant owners approve the next step.

Which alternatives may appear?

Expect regional patient-access or engagement platforms, enterprise health-cloud providers, AI receptionist vendors and FinOps tools. This is category context—not a ranking or recommendation.

How do we check cloud/API cost ownership?

Record account, service or model, driver, owner, baseline, confidence and safe action. Do not infer savings from a dashboard or change production settings without a stop rule.

Does this prove compliance or residency?

No. This is synthetic, no-PHI education. Confirm current local requirements, residency, contracts and security evidence with qualified advisers and internal owners.

How should we review a cross-border data path?

Map where data enters, is processed, stored, backed up and accessed. Record the accountable owner, retention rule, subprocessors and unresolved exceptions. Keep the review no-PHI until qualified local and internal owners approve the next step; this map does not establish compliance or residency.

Category context to compare, not a ranking

Buyer research may surface regional patient-access or engagement services such as Okadoc or Vezeeta, enterprise health-cloud references such as Oracle Health or AWS for Health, and cloud-economics approaches such as the FinOps Framework. These are category references, not endorsements or “top” claims. Compare every option using the same scope, data-path, evidence-owner, human-control and cost-accountability fields.

For local policy context, start with the UAE Government data-protection overview and obtain current Saudi requirements from the relevant official authority. This page does not interpret either.

Before you shortlist a vendor

  1. Can the vendor name the evidence owner, scope, date and unresolved questions for each security or AI claim?
  2. Can your team trace a patient enquiry from source to callback owner without exporting patient data?
  3. Are data-responsibility, retention, access and incident questions assigned to the right internal or external adviser?
  4. Can cloud-cost changes be reviewed by account, service owner, driver, confidence and stop rule?
  5. What must remain on hold until current primary evidence is reviewed?
