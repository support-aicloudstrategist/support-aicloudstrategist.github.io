Five evidence lanes

Turn a broad vendor conversation into reviewable ownership.

1. Patient-enquiry flow

Record the enquiry source, callback owner, handoff, response target and exception path without exporting patient data.

2. AI vendor questionnaire

Ask for evidence owner, version/date, scope, data path, subprocessors and unresolved questions before a demo becomes a commitment.

3. Data-responsibility questions

Separate operational questions from advice: who decides purpose, access, retention, incident escalation and review sign-off?

4. Cloud-cost ownership

Map account, cost pool, service owner, driver, confidence and safe next action before changing production settings.

5. Executive decision brief

Summarise Act, Investigate and Hold items with owner, due date, evidence link and stop rule.
