Evidence checklist before buying another intake or scheduling system

1. Referral intake register

Track referral source, enquiry time, service need, location, payer/process notes, owner, status and next action using the minimum necessary information.

2. Family enquiry follow-up

Separate missed calls, web forms, emails, WhatsApp or SMS follow-up, voicemail and unresolved questions so no family enquiry disappears between systems.

3. Start-of-care checklist

Map assessment scheduling, document requests, consent/notice prompts for review, internal handoffs and unresolved blockers before promising dates.

4. Caregiver scheduling exception queue

Track availability gaps, location fit, skill/language preferences, cancellations, replacements and owner escalations.

5. Human-review boundary

Route clinical, care-plan, eligibility, billing, emergency, safeguarding, staffing, privacy and compliance uncertainty to qualified people.

6. Weekly owner dashboard

Show open referrals, family enquiries, start-of-care blockers, caregiver scheduling exceptions, ageing and owner action.

Use this as a proof-before-platform filter

If referral intake, family follow-up and scheduling exceptions are not visible in one owner queue, more software may not fix the leakage.
