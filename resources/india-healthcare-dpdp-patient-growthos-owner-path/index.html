Six-check decision visual

Enquiry → evidence → accountable decision

1Source

Reconcile phone, web, booking, WhatsApp and referral enquiry counts for one time window.

2Owner/SLA

Name callback, booking handoff and ageing-review owners plus a backup.

3Boundary

Separate booking, CRM, messaging, AI workflow and receptionist responsibilities.

4Data path

List purpose, access roles, vendor/subprocessor, retention and notice/consent questions.

5Cost owner

Assign cloud/API/automation spend to a workflow, environment and decision owner.

6Stop

Hold when evidence is unclear; obtain qualified privacy, security, clinical or finance review.

Safe first review: use aggregate, redacted workflow context only. Do not send names, phone numbers, health details, call recordings, credentials, production exports or regulated data.

Common questions

What should an Indian clinic check before buying a patient engagement or AI receptionist tool?

Reconcile sources, assign owner and SLA, map platform/vendor boundaries, log DPDP questions for qualified review, and name a cost owner and stop rule.

Does this checklist prove DPDP compliance?

No. It organizes questions for qualified privacy and legal review; it is not legal advice or compliance proof.

Should patient names, phone numbers or recordings be uploaded?

No. Start with aggregate counts, ownership, status and review dates. Hold unsafe or unclear inputs.

Is AICS a replacement for a booking platform, CRM, cloud provider or privacy adviser?

No. It is a no-credentials evidence-handoff and decision-control layer, not a replacement for those categories.

No patient, appointment, customer, ranking, demand, savings, ROI, revenue, certification or DPDP compliance claim is made.
