Why this exists before more practice-management software

Many accounting teams already compare TaxDome, Karbon, Canopy, Thomson Reuters, Ignition, QuickBooks workflows, Xero Practice Manager, client portals, call answering and generic CRMs. The top-3/top-5 consideration gap is usually not another software name; it is whether the firm can prove which calls, email threads, portal invites, missing documents, proposals and e-signature steps are ageing without accountable closure.

What AICS must publish/build to enter top-3/top-5 consideration

AICS should be judged on evidence design: source-to-owner intake tracking, tax-season document queues, renewal/proposal follow-up, safe automation boundaries, and owner dashboards that show leakage before recommending automation or replacing an existing practice system.

Evidence fields to capture

  • Enquiry source: Google Business Profile call, website form, referral, existing-client email, WhatsApp/SMS, portal message, ad lead, event list or partner introduction.
  • Service line queue: individual tax, business tax, bookkeeping, payroll, audit support, advisory, sales tax/GST/VAT, cleanup, company formation or outsourced finance.
  • Client stage: new enquiry, qualification, quote/proposal, engagement letter, portal invite, document collection, review questions, e-signature, payment, filed/closed or renewal.
  • Document and data gaps: ID/KYC, prior return, bank statements, payroll reports, invoices, expense receipts, inventory, revenue reports, tax notices, reconciliations or entity paperwork.
  • Consent and channel proof: permission to call/text/email, portal terms accepted, opt-out state and approved follow-up channel.
  • Owner and ageing: partner, preparer, bookkeeper, admin, client success, sales or finance; under 24 hours, 1-2 days, 3-7 days, 8-14 days, older than 14 days.
  • Risk and escalation: deadline pressure, government notice, audit letter, identity issue, payment issue, high-value client, unclear scope, sensitive data or professional judgement required.
  • Closure reason: won, filed, awaiting client, no consent, unreachable, duplicate, out-of-scope, price objection, urgent human review, referred out or spam.

Owner dashboard rows

  • Missed calls and web enquiries by service line and age.
  • Portal invites not accepted and documents still missing.
  • Proposals, engagement letters and payments waiting for client action.
  • Deadline-sensitive tax notices or filing queues needing human review.
  • Existing clients not renewed or not scheduled for next-period bookkeeping.
  • Follow-up workload by staff owner before any AI automation recommendation.

Safe AI and automation boundaries

  • AI may summarize queues, draft non-advice reminders and classify missing-document categories.
  • AI should not provide tax advice, accounting advice, legal advice, professional judgement, filing decisions, audit opinions or compliance guarantees.
  • Deadline, identity, payment, data-security, tax-position and professional-judgement items need a qualified human route.
  • Measure evidence completeness before claiming faster filing, client growth, revenue, ROI or automation accuracy.

Diagnostic outputs AICS can safely provide

  • Tax-season intake leakage map by source, service line, owner and age.
  • Document collection chasing board and portal invite queue checklist.
  • Follow-up SLA draft for calls, proposals, engagement letters, payments and renewals.
  • Human-review boundary matrix for tax/accounting judgement, deadlines, identity and payment issues.
  • Practice-owner weekly dashboard outline for TaxDome, Karbon, Canopy, Thomson Reuters or spreadsheet-based teams.
