What we monitor every cycle

Website checks

Search and answer readiness

  • Canonical URLs, sitemap coverage and indexable pages.
  • JSON-LD validity for Organization, WebPage, Article, Breadcrumb and FAQ where visible.
  • LLMs/answer-engine files and clear extractable answers.
Conversion checks

Customer action paths

  • Every commercial page should point to contact, WhatsApp, phone or free review.
  • Broken internal links and wrong routes are treated as conversion blockers.
  • Owner-language CTAs are preferred over technical jargon.
Trust checks

Claims and proof honesty

  • No invented testimonials, client logos, certifications or partnerships.
  • Sample dashboards and audits must be clearly labelled as samples.
  • Legal/compliance content stays educational and review-gated.
Access needed

Third-party visibility

  • Google Search Console and Analytics require authenticated access.
  • Cloudflare visitor telemetry requires dashboard/API access.
  • External posting, backlinks and paid ads require login or approval.

How this helps customers

Many small and growing businesses do not know why their website receives visitors but still misses enquiries. The same monitoring logic can be applied to a customer website: check clear positioning, landing-page intent, proof safety, enquiry routes, WhatsApp follow-up, trust readiness, schema, and measurable leakage before recommending a sprint or managed system.

Before

Scattered pages, unclear claims, weak CTA routing, no monitoring habit and no evidence-backed improvement loop.

After

A practical scorecard, fixed technical gaps, safer trust messaging, stronger conversion routes and a clear list of blocked items that need owner access.

Current blockers we will not pretend are solved

  • Search Console performance and brand-search demand cannot be verified until access is available.
  • Google Analytics and Cloudflare visitor data cannot be verified from the public website alone.
  • External backlinks and social distribution must be based on real published URLs, not a plan.
  • Customer proof can only be added after real customers approve public use.
