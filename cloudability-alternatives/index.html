Why teams look for Cloudability alternatives

Cloudability (part of Apptio, an IBM company) is a full FinOps platform covering allocation, reporting, anomaly detection and rightsizing across clouds. Teams usually start looking at alternatives when platform cost feels heavy relative to cloud spend, reports exist but nobody acts on them, or ownership of shared cost was never set up.

A practical decision order

  1. Can you name an owner for each top spend line? If not, start there. This is the diagnostic’s job.
  2. Is Kubernetes allocation the main pain? Try OpenCost before paying for a platform.
  3. Is idle waste proven and owned? Automate clean-up with policy tooling.
  4. Do several business units need showback? Shortlist platforms (Cloudability included) and run a time-boxed pilot.
  5. Usage steady and ownership clear? Evaluate commitment automation last.

FAQ

Is AICloudStrategist a Cloudability replacement?

No. It is a fixed-fee ownership diagnostic, not a platform. It is the step before choosing, renewing or dropping a FinOps platform.

What are free alternatives to Cloudability?

Native consoles (Cost Explorer, Azure Cost Management, GCP Billing), OpenCost for Kubernetes allocation and Cloud Custodian for policy-driven clean-up. They carry no licence fee but need engineering time and clear owners.

Should we renew Cloudability or switch?

Check usage first: which reports drive decisions, who acts on them, and which spend lines are still unowned. If decisions are not being made, a different tool will not fix that.

Do you take a share of savings?

No. The diagnostic is a fixed fee after scope.
