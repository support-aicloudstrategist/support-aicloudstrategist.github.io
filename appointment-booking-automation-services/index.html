The booking page is rarely the whole problem. The real leak is often a missed call, a form lead that waits overnight, a WhatsApp chat with no owner, a no-show that never gets a second touch, or a calendar event that never updates the CRM. AICS designs the operating loop around those leaks.

What gets automated

Missed-call capture

Create callback tasks, qualification notes and booking prompts when the phone is busy, closed or unanswered.

Form-to-calendar handoff

Route website enquiries into a clear next step: booking link, callback queue, staff owner or CRM stage.

WhatsApp and email reminders

Set consent-aware reminder and confirmation flows without spammy bulk-message behaviour or unsupported delivery claims.

No-show follow-up

Record no-shows, trigger a reschedule task and show unresolved appointments in an owner dashboard.

CRM and pipeline updates

Keep appointment status, source, owner, outcome and next action visible instead of scattered across inboxes.

Human escalation rules

Use automation for reminders and routing while keeping judgement, sensitive questions and exceptions with the team.

FAQ

Can AICS work with Calendly, Google Calendar, Microsoft 365, CRM tools or WhatsApp?

Usually yes, depending on access, permissions, API limits and platform rules. The first step is to map the current tool stack and identify the smallest safe integration path.

Is this only for healthcare?

No. Healthcare, education, real estate, consultants and local services can all use booking automation. Healthcare workflows need extra care around privacy, medical claims and human escalation.

Do you send outreach or bulk messages?

No. This service is for inbound enquiry and appointment workflows. Customer sends, outreach and paid campaigns require separate approval and must follow platform and consent rules.
