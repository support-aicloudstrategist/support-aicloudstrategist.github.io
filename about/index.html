About AICloudStrategist

Cost governance for accountable cloud and AI decisions.

AICloudStrategist is a professional consultancy focused on one primary service: the Cloud & AI Cost Governance Diagnostic.

Operating positionEvidence before action

The work connects cost, workload context, ownership, constraints and client-controlled decisions without promising automatic savings.

Why the company exists

Cloud and AI spending should remain explainable.

Modern technology costs can cross providers, products, clusters, models, teams and business units. AICS helps organizations establish a decision-ready view before cost actions are approved.

Clarity

Make the service, scope, evidence and intended decision understandable before introducing complexity.

Accountability

Connect material observations and actions to accepted business and technical ownership.

Restraint

Do not treat every cost increase as waste or every recommendation as safe to implement.

Traceability

Retain sources, assumptions, confidence, limitations and unresolved gaps in the delivered record.

Institutional evidence standard

Public proof must identify what it is—and what it is not.

Current public artifacts demonstrate methods and decision structures. They do not stand in for approved customer outcomes.

01Verified evidenceIdentify the source, scope, verifier and limitations. Self-verification must remain explicit.
02Representative evidenceA synthetic artifact showing structure or method without implying customer delivery.
03Simulated methodA controlled demonstration that does not claim real-world performance.
04Unresolved evidenceMaterial gaps remain visible instead of being converted into certainty.
