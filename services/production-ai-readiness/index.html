Fixed scope

One review across five production decisions.

Evaluation evidence

Success criteria, failure modes, regression checks, human review and unsupported claims.

Authority & data

What the system can see, decide, invoke and change; access and sensitive-data boundaries.

Operations & economics

Monitoring, incident ownership, rollback paths, consumption signals and operating cost assumptions.

Deliverables

Evidence for a release, remediation or hold decision.

  • Production readiness dossier
  • Risk, control and evidence gap register
  • Human authority and escalation matrix
  • Release conditions and 30-day action plan
  • Executive readout with named owners

Delivery boundary

Initial scoping uses architecture, policies, test summaries and redacted evidence. Do not send credentials, secrets, customer data or regulated records through the public form.

This diagnostic is not a certification, penetration test, legal opinion, compliance attestation or guarantee of system safety.
