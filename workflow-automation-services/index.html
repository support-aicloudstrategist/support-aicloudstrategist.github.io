FAQ

Do you guarantee staff time savings or revenue growth?

No. AICS does not guarantee savings, revenue, ranking, compliance or AI accuracy. We document the starting workflow, implement a measurable improvement and keep claims inside observed evidence.

Can this work with spreadsheets and WhatsApp?

Often yes. Many first automations use the current website, email, WhatsApp, CRM-lite tracker, spreadsheet, calendar or project board before a larger platform is justified.

What should not be fully automated?

Customer promises, pricing exceptions, legal or compliance statements, medical or financial advice, identity-sensitive actions, payments and irreversible customer sends should stay human-reviewed.
