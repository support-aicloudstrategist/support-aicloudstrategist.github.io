Data handling

Evidence moves only after fit—and only by agreement.

Public pages and forms never receive production credentials, raw billing exports, or regulated personal data. Confidential material uses a separate, agreed channel.

Principles

What we ask for—and what we refuse on the website.

01

No credentials here

Cloud console logins, API keys, kubeconfigs, and service principals are not collected through this site or the fit-review form.

02

Export-first evidence

After fit, we prefer client-generated billing exports (CUR, Cost Management, Billing CSV/Parquet) and redacted architecture summaries.

03

Redaction by default

Account names, resource IDs, and customer identifiers can be pseudonymized when your policy requires it. We document what was redacted.

04

Mutual NDA

Before confidential exports move, we sign a mutual NDA or work under your standard vendor NDA—whichever procurement requires.

05

Retention

Working copies are kept only for the engagement period plus a short wind-down window stated in the proposal. Deletion on request after delivery.

06

Read-only posture

The diagnostic is advisory. We do not execute production changes or deploy agents as part of standard scope.

Fit review

The free call needs no billing files.

Share the business decision and rough estate shape only. If we proceed, evidence access is scoped in writing with format, period, and retention limits.
