Most cloud cost optimization programs fail for the same reason: teams cut before they know who owns each dollar. AICloudStrategist runs a fixed-fee Cloud & AI Cost Governance Diagnostic that connects AWS, Azure, GCP, Kubernetes and AI/GPU spend to accountable owners first—then classifies the next 30 days as act, investigate or hold.

What cloud cost optimization should produce

  • Owned spend map — assigned, shared and unallocated cost with named owners.
  • Decision register — act / investigate / hold with confidence and risk notes.
  • 30-day action plan — prioritized moves you own; we do not execute production changes as part of the diagnostic.

When this beats another dashboard

Platforms show lines on a bill. Optimization without ownership becomes politics. If your finance and engineering teams disagree on idle Kubernetes, GPU pools or shared platform cost, start with ownership mapping—not another recommendation feed.
