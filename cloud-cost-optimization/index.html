Buyer questions

Common cost questions answered plainly.

What is cloud cost optimization without a savings guarantee?

It means finding evidence-backed actions—rightsizing, waste removal, commitment coverage and ownership fixes—then letting your team approve changes. No fixed ROI or savings percentage is promised.

Does this cover multi-cloud cost management?

Yes where the agreed estate includes more than one provider. The diagnostic reconciles the included billing period across the scoped accounts and shared platforms.

How is this different from a cloud cost calculator?

Calculators estimate list-price math. The diagnostic uses your estate evidence, owners and constraints to decide which actions are safe.

What does the diagnostic cost?

Fit review is free. Typical single-estate work is USD 2,500–3,000 internationally or INR 1,00,000–1,50,000 in India, confirmed in writing after scope.
