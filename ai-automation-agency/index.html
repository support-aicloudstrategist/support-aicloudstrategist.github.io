How an AICS automation sprint works

  1. Revenue leak map: identify where leads, appointments, payments, support or trust handoffs slow down.
  2. Tool fit check: review existing website, CRM, calendar, email, WhatsApp and reporting setup before adding new tools.
  3. Workflow design: define trigger, owner, message, deadline, escalation and measurement boundary.
  4. Build and test: implement the smallest reliable automation that creates business value.
  5. Proof and reporting: document what changed, what was measured and what remains outside the claim boundary.
